## Multi-Perspective Analysis Framework

Swedish parliamentary reporting requires analyzing each development from multiple stakeholder perspectives. Use this framework to ensure comprehensive coverage.

### The Six Perspectives

Apply all six perspectives to significant parliamentary developments:

#### 1. Government Perspective
**Question**: How does this serve or challenge the Tidö coalition's agenda?
- Ministerial response and official position
- Coalition alignment (M, KD, L) and SD support confidence
- Implementation pathway and budget implications
- Communication strategy and public framing

#### 2. Opposition Perspective
**Question**: How does the main opposition (S, V, MP) respond and what alternatives do they propose?
- S (Social Democrats) position as largest opposition party
- V (Left Party) ideological critique
- MP (Green Party) environmental/social angle
- Opposition unity or fragmentation on this issue

#### 3. Citizen Perspective
**Question**: How does this affect Swedish residents in daily life?
- Direct policy impact (taxes, services, rights)
- Vulnerable group considerations (elderly, unemployed, immigrants)
- Democratic access (transparency, participation rights)
- Timeline to impact (immediate, medium-term, long-term)

#### 4. Economic Perspective
**Question**: What are the fiscal and market implications?
- Budget impact (SEK amounts where available from SCB/World Bank)
- Business sector effects (Swedish Trade Federation, employer organizations)
- Labor market implications (union positions, employment data)
- Municipal/regional economic effects

#### 5. International Perspective
**Question**: How does this relate to EU, Nordic, and global contexts?
- EU directive compliance or conflict
- Nordic Council comparison (similar policies in Denmark, Norway, Finland)
- OECD comparison for policy benchmarking
- Foreign policy implications if relevant

#### 6. Media/Public Discourse Perspective
**Question**: What is the public narrative and media framing?
- Parliamentary debate tone and rhetoric
- Social media/public attention level
- Historical precedents and comparison
- Controversy or consensus framing

### Applying Perspectives

**For all articles**: Always apply Government + Opposition + Citizen perspectives
**For budget/economic articles**: Add Economic + International perspectives
**For deep-inspection articles**: Apply all six perspectives
**For breaking news**: Lead with Government + Citizen, add others as available

### Perspective Attribution Standards

Never generalize perspectives. Always attribute to:
- Named politicians (full name + party)
- Official party documents (dok_id)
- Organizational statements (Swedish Trade Federation, LO, etc.)
- Statistical data (SCB table ID, World Bank indicator)

### Common Perspective Pitfalls

❌ "The opposition criticized..." (which party? which politician?)
❌ "Citizens are concerned..." (which citizens? what evidence?)
❌ "Economically speaking..." (what numbers? what sources?)
❌ Balanced-sounding language that actually favors one perspective
❌ Missing opposition perspective (both coalition AND opposition must appear)

### Required Balance Standards

Every article with policy analysis must include:
- ≥ 2 parties cited by name
- ≥ 1 government/coalition position
- ≥ 1 opposition position
- ≥ 1 citizen/impact angle
- ≥ 1 forward-looking implication

Failure to meet these standards = insufficient perspective coverage.
