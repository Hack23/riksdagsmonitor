Key Actors

Social Democrats (S): 13 interpellations — dominant scrutiny actor, building election narrative on regional neglect and integration failure. Key MPs: Lawen Redar (3 interpellations on integration/police), Mikael Dahlqvist (2 on regional transport), Peter Hultqvist (defence-infrastructure nexus).

Left Party (V): 2 interpellations — focused disability rights offensive via Nadja Awad targeting both M and KD ministers on social welfare.

Ministerial Accountability Scorecard

Andreas Carlson (KD) — Infrastructure Minister — faces 7 interpellations (most of any minister): transport, housing, accessibility, defence infrastructure. Response deadline: April 21–27.

Elisabeth Svantesson (M) — Finance Minister — faces 3 interpellations: Postnord governance, integration economics, assistance compensation.

Camilla Waltersson Grönvall (M) — Social Services Minister — faces 2 interpellations: disability rights (Funktionsrätt review, LSS failures).

7 other ministers face 1 interpellation each, spanning justice, foreign affairs, labour, energy, healthcare, and civil affairs.

Political Impact

With 428 interpellations filed in the 2025/26 session, the opposition is running a systematic accountability campaign ahead of the September 2026 election. The concentration of pressure on Infrastructure Minister Carlson (KD) — 7 of 15 recent interpellations — suggests a deliberate strategy to expose what the opposition frames as government neglect of regional Sweden. The Tidö coalition's 88.5% voting discipline indicates policy unity, but ministerial response quality under this volume pressure remains the key variable.

While the government coalition maintains strong voting discipline, opposition parties have mounted a coordinated multi-front scrutiny campaign targeting infrastructure, integration, and social welfare.
