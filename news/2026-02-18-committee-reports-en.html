Road traffic and vehicle issues

Committee: TU

Document: HD01TU9

The Transport Committee proposes that the Riksdag rejects approximately 120 proposals on road traffic and vehicle issues from the 2025 general motion period. The proposals deal with, among other things, work towards a fossil-free vehicle fleet, access to fossil-free fuelling, charging infrastructure for electric vehicles, and the organisation of road maintenance and winter road management. The committee refers to planned or already implemented measures and ongoing work.
