Opposition Strategy

The Social Democrats (S) lead the charge with 8 motions across six committees — from renewable energy (NU) and macroprudential policy (FiU) to housing regulation (CU), waste reform (MJU), taxation (SkU) and public procurement (FiU). This breadth-first strategy reflects a party preparing to govern, not merely to oppose.

The Green Party (MP) contributes 5 motions focused on environmental and civil liberties issues — renewables licensing, waste reform, housing fraud, elderly care language requirements and security detention — consistent with their dual emphasis on green transition and individual rights.

The Centre Party (C) has filed 4 motions covering energy, environment, justice and constitutional affairs, maintaining their centrist pivot that bridges economic liberalism with rights-based arguments.

The Left Party (V) contributes 3 motions on elderly care, public procurement and constitutional rights, underscoring their consistent focus on workers' rights, public services and civil liberties.
