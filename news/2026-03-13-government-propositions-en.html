Published: 2026-03-10

This proposition concerns government bill 2025/26:167 — Kärnämneskontroll och bedömningar av överensstämmelse med säkerhets- och strålskyddskrav i verksamhet med strålning. Submitted by Johan Britz (Klimat- och näringslivsdepartementet), the proposal addresses nuclear materials controls and radiation safety assessments.

Why It Matters: Part of the government's comprehensive nuclear energy expansion package. Updated nuclear materials controls and radiation safety assessments are essential prerequisites before any new reactors can be approved, making this a foundational piece of Sweden's energy transition strategy.

Read the full proposition: HD03167

Ny kärnkraft i Sverige – fler möjliga platser vid kusten

Published: 2026-03-10

This proposition concerns government bill 2025/26:160 — Ny kärnkraft i Sverige – fler möjliga platser vid kusten. Submitted by Johan Britz (Klimat- och näringslivsdepartementet), the proposal opens new coastal sites for nuclear power plants in Sweden.

Why It Matters: This is the centrepiece of the government's nuclear agenda — opening new coastal sites for nuclear power plants marks a historic reversal of Sweden's decades-long nuclear phase-out policy. The proposal will face scrutiny in the Environment and Energy Committee (MJU) and is likely to become a key election issue.

Read the full proposition: HD03160

Why It Matters: Modernising safety and radiation protection rules for uranium extraction and processing enables Sweden to develop domestic nuclear fuel supply chains. This complements the new reactor siting proposal and signals the government's ambition for full-cycle nuclear sovereignty.

Effektiv och säker byggprocess

Published: 2026-03-10

This proposition concerns government bill 2025/26:172 — Effektiv och säker byggprocess. Submitted by Andreas Carlson (Landsbygds- och infrastrukturdepartementet), the proposal streamlines the construction permitting process for faster and safer building.

Why It Matters: Sweden's housing shortage remains one of the country's most pressing domestic challenges. Streamlining the building process aims to reduce construction timelines and costs, directly affecting housing supply targets. The Civil Affairs Committee (CU) review will determine whether safety standards are maintained while cutting red tape.

Read the full proposition: HD03172

Lag om avgift för områdessamverkan

Published: 2026-03-10

This proposition concerns government bill 2025/26:157 — Lag om avgift för områdessamverkan. Submitted by Andreas Carlson (Landsbygds- och infrastrukturdepartementet), the proposal introduces a new fee for area cooperation (Business Improvement Districts).

Why It Matters: Business Improvement Districts (BIDs) have been debated in Sweden for years. This new mandatory area cooperation levy could transform urban commercial centres by enabling coordinated investment in safety, cleanliness, and amenities — but critics warn it amounts to a new tax on property owners.

Read the full proposition: HD03157

Why It Matters: Expanded background checks for school personnel respond to growing public concern about child safety.
