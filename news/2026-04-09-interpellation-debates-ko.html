Sweden Democrats have filed two high-profile interpellations challenging coalition partners on constitutional rights and religious extremism, testing the Tidö Agreement coalition’s unity as the 2026 election approaches. SD’s Rashid Farivar questions Justice Minister Gunnar Strömmer (M) on whether proposition 2025/26:133 enables a “violent’s veto” undermining Sweden’s 1766 press freedom tradition, while Richard Jomshof demands Social Minister Jakob Forssmed (KD) act on antisemitic hate preaching in Kristianstad mosques exposed by Expressen.
