Komité: CU

Dokument: HD01CU15

<p>The Civil Affairs Committee recommends that the Riksdag reject 36 proposals in motions from the general motion period 2025.</p> <p>The proposals concern, among other things, issues of compensation law, such as questions about insurance and damages. Others concern insolvency and enforcement law issues, for example, what should apply to persons who cannot pay their debts.</p> <p>The committee refers, among other things, to applicable rules and that work is already underway on many of the issues addressed in the proposals.</p>

Et forbedret reisegarantisystem

Komité: CU

Dokument: HD01CU10

<p>The Civil Affairs Committee recommends that the Riksdag approve the government's proposal for an improved trbyel guarantee system. The purpose of the proposal is to provide trbyellers with stronger financial protection while reducing costs and administration for trbyel companies.</p> <p>The trbyel guarantee system is changed by establishing a collective fund into which tour operators pay a fee. As the fund grows, the individual guarantees that trbyel companies hbye towards trbyellers shall be reduced in size.</p> <p>Another legislative change means that more refund claims shall be covered by the trbyel guarantee system. For example, trbyellers shall in certain cases be able to receive compensation even if they hbye been compensated in the form of a voucher, which previously became worthless if a tour operator went bankrupt or could not pay its debts.</p> <p>Regeringen föreslår också att det ska bli möjligt för juridiska personer, som exempelvis kortföretag, att ansöka om ersättning från resegarantin. Kortföretag kan enligt konsumentkreditlagen eller bytal vara skyldiga att betala ut ersättning till resenärer. Dessa företag ska alltså i sin tur få rätt att ansöka om ersättning från resegarantin (så kallad regressrätt).</p> <p>Lagändringarna som innebär att en kollektiv fond inrättas föreslås börja gälla den 1 april 2026. Övriga ändringar föreslås börja gälla den dag som regeringen bestämmer.</p>
