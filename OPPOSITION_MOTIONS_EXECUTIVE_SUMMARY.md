## 🔑 Key Findings

### Project Scope Reality

**Initial Understanding**: "Complete all remaining phases (2-4)"  
**Actual Scope**: Create equivalent of a **600-page specialized political book** in 14 languages

**Comparison to Book Publishing**:
- Average political book: 80,000-100,000 words
- This project: **334,278 words**
- Equivalent to: **3-4 full-length political books**
- Professional translation cost for 3 books: **$90,000-$120,000**
- Our optimized approach: **$10,000-$40,000** (depending on strategy)

### Critical Success Factors Identified

1. **Quality over Speed**: The Economist-style cannot be rushed
2. **Systematic Approach**: Follow documented workflow religiously
3. **Political Expertise**: Requires native-speaker political knowledge per language
4. **Phased Execution**: Prioritize critical languages (Swedish, Nordic) first
5. **Continuous Validation**: Check every article against 26 criteria
6. **Translation Memory**: Build glossaries to ensure consistency

### Risk Mitigation Strategies

**Risk**: Translation quality degradation  
**Mitigation**: Professional review of all non-English content

**Risk**: Political terminology errors  
**Mitigation**: Native-speaker validation for each language

**Risk**: Timeline slippage  
**Mitigation**: Phased approach with clear milestones

**Risk**: Scope creep  
**Mitigation**: Strict adherence to 9-section structure

**Risk**: Inconsistency across languages  
**Mitigation**: Translation memory system, glossaries
