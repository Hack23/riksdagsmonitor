  • Politician-Level (20 rules): Attendance, voting consistency, role conflicts, productivity

📊 Citizen Intelligence Agency Data Products

Riksdagsmonitor is powered by the Citizen Intelligence Agency (CIA) — an open-source OSINT platform that processes Swedish parliamentary data into actionable intelligence products.

📈 Risk Assessment Reports

45 risk rules across 4 domains analyze politician behavior, party discipline, committee effectiveness, and ministry accountability in real-time.

📅 Seasonal Activity Analysis

Z-score anomaly detection identifies unusual parliamentary activity patterns by quarter, with historical baselines from 2002 to present.

🎯 Election Cycle Intelligence

Performance tracking across 9+ election cycles (1994–2034) with predictive risk forecasting, party tier analysis, and coalition probability modeling.

🔗 Open Data Integration

All intelligence products are derived from official open data sources. Visualizations update automatically via nightly data pipelines with 24-hour caching.

All data products available through the Citizen Intelligence Agency platform with real-time updates from Swedish Parliament open data APIs.

🔗 Data Sources & Integration

Riksdagsmonitor integrates multiple authoritative Swedish and international data sources:

Swedish Parliament (Riksdagen)

Official parliamentary data including votes, documents, committee work, and MP information.

data.riksdagen.se

Swedish Election Authority

Historical election results, voter turnout, and electoral statistics for all national elections.

val.se

Financial Management Authority (ESV)

Government budget data, public spending analysis, and financial accountability reporting.

esv.se/psidata

World Bank Open Data

Country-level economic and development indicators for international comparative analysis.

data.worldbank.org
