Frequently Asked Questions

What is Riksdagsmonitor?

Riksdagsmonitor is an open-source Swedish Parliament intelligence platform that monitors votes, motions, propositions, committee reports and government actions in real time across 14 languages.

How often is the sitemap updated?

This HTML sitemap is regenerated on every deploy (multiple times per day). The machine-readable XML sitemap is published at /sitemap.xml and refreshes on the same cadence.

How do I switch languages?

Use the language switcher in the header or the flag links at the bottom of every section. Each of the 14 supported languages has its own sitemap (sitemap_sv.html etc.) with `hreflang` annotations.

Where do I find the XML sitemap?

The machine-readable XML sitemap is at /sitemap.xml and the search-engine crawler instructions live in /robots.txt. Both are linked from the "Additional Resources" section above.

Are the article sources auditable?

Yes. Every article links to the underlying analysis artifacts on GitHub (analysis/daily/YYYY-MM-DD/stream/) so any reader can audit the OSINT chain end-to-end.
