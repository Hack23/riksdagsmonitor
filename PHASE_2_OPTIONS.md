## Constraint Analysis

### What Can Be Automated ✅

**Technical Infrastructure:**
- HTML file structure and templates

### What Requires Human Expertise ❌

**Linguistic Quality:**
- Professional political journalism translation
- Cultural adaptation for Danish/Norwegian/Finnish readers
- Accurate political terminology (Nordic political systems)
- The Economist editorial style preservation
- Context appropriate for each Nordic country
- Nuanced policy analysis translation

### Why Professional Translation is Required

**1. Language Complexity:**
- **Danish/Norwegian:** North Germanic (related to Swedish but distinct)
- **Finnish:** Finno-Ugric language family (entirely different from Swedish)
- Political terminology varies significantly between countries

**2. Political Context:**
- Swedish political institutions need Nordic equivalents
- Coalition dynamics differ (Denmark, Norway, Finland have unique systems)
- EU/international context varies by country
- Historical references require cultural adaptation

**3. Editorial Standards:**
- The Economist analytical depth must be preserved
- Sophisticated prose style in target language
- Multiple perspectives and balanced reporting
- Fact-dense reporting with local relevance
