### Sensitivity Levels

| Level | Code | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Public** | PUB | Publicly available parliamentary data | Published propositions, public votes, committee reports |
| **Restricted** | RES | Limited distribution information | Internal party discussions reported in media, leaked documents |
| **Confidential** | CON | Sensitive political intelligence | Unpublished coalition negotiations, diplomatic considerations |

### Impact Levels

| Level | Score | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | 4 | Fundamental governance change | Government collapse, constitutional amendment, snap election |
| **High** | 3 | Significant policy shift | Major legislation, minister resignation, coalition realignment |
| **Medium** | 2 | Notable political development | Committee report, new motion, policy debate |
| **Low** | 1 | Routine parliamentary activity | Standard questions, minor procedural matters |

### Urgency Levels

| Level | Code | Response Time | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Breaking** | BRK | Immediate (< 1 hour) | Unprecedented events requiring immediate coverage |
| **Major** | MAJ | Same day (< 8 hours) | Significant developments requiring rapid analysis |
| **Standard** | STD | Next cycle (< 24 hours) | Regular political developments for daily analysis |
| **Background** | BKG | Weekly synthesis | Context items for trend analysis |

### Priority Calculation

Overall Priority = Impact Score × Urgency Multiplier

| Urgency | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Breaking | ×4 |
| Major | ×3 |
| Standard | ×2 |
| Background | ×1 |

**Priority Thresholds:**
- **Immediate** (12-16): Breaking news, top article priority
- **High** (8-11): Same-day article, prominent placement
- **Standard** (4-7): Regular article inclusion
- **Monitor** (1-3): Background tracking, weekly summary
