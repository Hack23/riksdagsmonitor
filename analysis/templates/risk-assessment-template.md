## Risk Scoring Methodology

Adapted from the ISMS Risk Assessment Methodology (Likelihood × Impact):

### Probability Scale

| Level | Score | Description | Political Context |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Very Likely** | 4 | Expected to occur | Legislative vote scheduled, policy announced |
| **Likely** | 3 | High probability | Strong political signals, coalition discussions |
| **Possible** | 2 | Could occur | Media speculation, informal discussions |
| **Unlikely** | 1 | Low probability | Contradicts current political dynamics |

### Impact Scale

| Level | Score | Description | Political Context |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | 4 | Fundamental change | Government collapse, snap election, constitutional change |
| **High** | 3 | Significant effect | Major policy reversal, coalition realignment, minister resignation |
| **Medium** | 2 | Moderate effect | Committee report, new motion, party position shift |
| **Low** | 1 | Minor effect | Routine parliamentary business, minor statements |

### Risk Score Thresholds

| Score Range | Category | Response |
|---|---|---|
| 12–16 | **Critical** | Breaking news, immediate deep analysis |
| 8–11 | **High** | Major article, same-day coverage |
| 4–7 | **Medium** | Standard article inclusion |
| 1–3 | **Low** | Background monitoring, weekly synthesis |
