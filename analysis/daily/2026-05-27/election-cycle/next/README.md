This directory contains the complete forward-scenarios political intelligence analysis for the **next** Swedish parliamentary mandate (2026-2030), generated on 2026-05-27.

## Key Finding

**Sweden faces a historically open election. Centerpartiet is the decisive kingmaker, and SD's potential formal government entry is the mandate's most consequential unknown.** The most likely single outcome is Scenario B (S-led minority, 35-40%), but the combined A scenarios (45%) remain viable. No scenario achieves a comfortable parliamentary majority without C's active cooperation.
