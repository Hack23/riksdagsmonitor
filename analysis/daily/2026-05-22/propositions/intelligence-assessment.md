## Key Judgments (KJs)

### KJ-1 — The Migration Cluster Represents a Constitutional Threshold Crossing (HIGH)

Sweden's five migration propositions collectively cross a constitutional threshold that has not been tested since the 1989 Aliens Act. The permanent abolition of a legal status category (HD03262), combined with automated SÄPO-triggered deportation (HD03267), constitutes a structural shift from a rights-based to a revocable-status model of immigration governance. This shift is deliberate, coherent, and designed to be difficult to reverse regardless of which government holds office post-2026.

**Confidence**: HIGH — based on text of HD03262 and HD03263 (primary sources); consistent with 4-year SD supply-and-confidence agreement objectives; no contradictory evidence.

**Key assumption**: The proposition texts as submitted reflect the final government position. If Lagrådet demands fundamental revision, this judgment would need to be reassessed.

### KJ-2 — Centerpartiet's Position on HD03262 is the Single Most Important Variable (HIGH)

The outcome of the entire batch — including the other nine propositions — depends disproportionately on whether Centerpartiet's parliamentary group maintains discipline. The government's working majority of 175/349 seats allows zero defections if SD supports all propositions. C's three dissenting MPs represent exactly the margin that could collapse the HD03262 committee vote. The probability distribution of all four scenarios is entirely driven by C's June 2026 formal position.

**Confidence**: HIGH — based on SfU seat mathematics (9 members, government 5 vs opposition 4 before C split), prior voting records, and PIR-6 tracking.

**Priority Intelligence Requirement**: PIR-6 — C position on HD03262 by 2026-06-15.

### KJ-3 — HD03267 Will Face Strasbourg Suspension in the First Enforcement Wave (MEDIUM)

Sweden's experience with ECtHR interim measures (14 suspensions since 2022 on deportation cases) and the structural parallel with UK SIAC cases (UK lost 7/11 in Article 3 proceedings) provide convergent evidence that the first cohort of HD03267 deportations — particularly to countries with documented SÄPO-designated threat profiles in MENA, Central Asia, or Sub-Saharan Africa — will be suspended by ECtHR pending examination. This is not a fatal blow to the legislation but will significantly reduce its electoral impact if it happens before September 2026.

**Confidence**: MEDIUM — outcome is structurally likely based on ECHR jurisprudence but timing (before vs after election) is uncertain and depends on which countries HD03267 deportations target first.

### KJ-4 — The Digital Identity Cluster Will Outlast All Migration Propositions (HIGH)

HD03250 (state e-identity) and HD03261 (Skatteverket registry) will survive any post-election government change because they serve administrative efficiency rather than partisan ideology. A Social Democratic government would not reverse a state digital identity system any more than a Conservative Danish government reversed Danish NemID. These two measures represent the most durable governance legacy of the Busch batch, independent of electoral outcome.

**Confidence**: HIGH — no post-election political constituency for reversal; S party platform does not call for reversal of HD03261 or HD03250; Denmark's NemID precedent confirms durability.

### KJ-5 — The Pre-Election Legislative Window Will Close in Late June 2026 (MEDIUM)

The Riksdag summer recess begins in late June 2026. For the full batch to pass before the election, all ten propositions must complete committee stage and plenary vote by approximately 2026-06-20. At current pace, this is feasible for eight of ten propositions but requires committee calendar acceleration for HD03254 (KU review risk) and HD03267 (Lagrådet consultation). If the window closes with HD03254 or HD03267 unresolved, they pass in autumn plenary — after the election, under a potentially different government.

**Confidence**: MEDIUM — Riksdag calendar is published but committee scheduling discretion is wide; no reliable source for exact SfU/JuU committee vote dates.
