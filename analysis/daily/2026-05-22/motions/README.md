## Overview

This analysis covers the most significant opposition motions filed in Riksdagen during 2025/26, with particular focus on motions filed on or immediately before 2026-05-21. The dominant thematic clusters are:

1. **Security & Surveillance**: V motions against expanded security measures for foreign nationals (JuU) and extended biometric data powers for Skatteverket (SkU)
2. **Migration & Detention**: Multiple opposition motions (V, C, S) against abolition of permanent residence permits and stricter detention rules (SfU)
3. **Transparency & Economic Data**: S and MP motions for more comprehensive household debt/asset registry; C and S motions against proposed disclosure rules for union political contributions (KU, FiU)
4. **EU Foreign Policy**: MP motions against EU partnership agreements with Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan (UU)
