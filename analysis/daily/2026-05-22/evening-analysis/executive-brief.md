## Critical Intelligence Points

### 1. Education Reform Package — High Significance
Three UbU committee reports issued simultaneously:
- **UbU27** (Bättre förutsättningar för yrkesutbildning): Vocational exams replace final projects on vocational programmes from 1 July 2026/2 July 2028. Expanded entrepreneurship in teaching. Signals government priority for workforce-aligned education.
- **UbU22** (Trygghet och studiero): Compulsory mobile phone collection at school start, new school rules framework with explicit consequence plans, reinforced disciplinary powers including entry denial for threatening students. Implementation date: July 2026.
- **UbU19** (Riksrevisionen): Committee endorses Riksrevisionen's finding that state efforts on evidence-based education are "not efficient." Government accepts the critique, creating audit accountability chain.

### 2. Procurement Reform — Medium-High Significance
- **FiU42** (Förenklad leverantörskontroll): Coordinated registry verification system for public procurement and freedom of choice systems. Addresses long-standing EU compliance gap on supplier exclusion criteria. Reduces compliance burden on contracting authorities. Aligns with EU transparency requirements.

### 3. Security Deportation — High Political Significance
- **SfU37** (Skärpta villkor för anhöriginvandring): Tightened family reunification conditions; scheduled for August 2026 decision — strategically placed in post-election period.
- **HD024192** (MP counter-motion): MP directly opposes prop. 2025/26:267 (qualified security threats), specifically challenging provisions allowing continued or expanded detention of children. Human rights framing versus security imperative.
- **HD024191** (MP counter-motion): MP opposes Skatteverket expanded folkbokföring powers, demanding faster safeguard legislation.

### 4. Consumer Credit Law — Medium Significance
- **CU26**: New konsumentkreditlag implementing EU Directive. Interest/cost caps, stricter credit assessment, new licensing requirements for lenders. Effective November 2026.

### 5. Foreign Affairs (Russia-Ukraine Context)
- **UU12** (Europarådet): Period characterised by "continued serious security situation" from Russia's full-scale war on Ukraine. Parliamentary delegation maintained strong focus on Ukraine support and accountability.
- **UU11** (OSSE): Committee underscores Sweden's active OSSE support role for European security order.
