## Summary

Today's parliamentary activity marks a pivotal pre-election moment in Swedish politics. The Tidö Coalition (M–SD–KD–L) has advanced a legislative sprint with five major thematic clusters:

1. **Migration Architecture**: The capstone HD03262–HD03265 bundle completes Sweden's decade-long transformation of asylum policy — permanent residence permits eliminated, compulsory departure obligations reinforced, identity documentation tightened, and international protective status reforms applied.

2. **Nuclear Energy**: HD01NU19 bypasses miljöbalken for nuclear licensing with direct government approval authority — the most structurally significant energy policy shift since the 2022 nuclear reversal.

3. **Citizenship Tightening**: HD01SfU28 extends residency requirement from 5 to 8 years and introduces mandatory language and civic knowledge tests — first time citizenship requirements approach German/Dutch levels.

4. **Crime Accountability**: HD10458 (Ib Isaksson, S) forces the government to defend its September 2022 "eradicate gang crime within a legislative term" promise against record 2025 explosion statistics. HD01FöU13 tightens explosives licensing as a tactical response.

5. **Transparency and Governance**: HD01KU39 (increased transparency in political processes), HD01FiU49 (state debt management evaluation), and pending interpellations on agency activism (HD10459) collectively probe the coalition's administrative accountability record.
