## Thematic Clusters

### Cluster 1: Migration Reform Package (HD03262–HD03265) — DOMINANT
The most significant legislative cluster of this session. Four interlocking propositions implement the EU Migration and Asylum Pact and constitute the most comprehensive tightening of Swedish asylum and migration rules since 2015-2016. This is SD's primary policy achievement delivered through M-led government.

**Lead proposition**: HD03262 — Elimination of permanent residence permits (replaces all prior temporary/permanent distinctions) + transposition of EU Asylum Procedures Regulation (APR), Qualification Regulation (QR), Reception Conditions Directive (RCD), Return Directive. Marks fundamental philosophical shift: Sweden moves from integration-track to return-track as default.

**Supporting propositions**:
- HD03263: Operational return/deportation infrastructure — new legal tools for Migrationsverket and police
- HD03264: Conduct-based revocation — criminal behaviour during residence triggers permit loss
- HD03265: Expanded detention powers — longer detention periods, electronic monitoring, broader grounds

### Cluster 2: NATO/Defence Deepening (HD03254)
Post-NATO-accession operational framework. Enables Swedish forces to operate under foreign command, accept foreign forces on Swedish territory, participate in combined NATO operations without ad-hoc parliamentary authorisation for each operation. Significant constitutional dimension (war powers delegation to government).

### Cluster 3: Transparency/Integrity Reform (HD03258)
Pre-election transparency push: increased public disclosure requirements for political parties, financing transparency, lobbying registers. Election-sensitive: government seeks to lock in transparency rules before September vote.

### Cluster 4: Welfare/Science (HD03251, HD03260)
Administrative reforms. HD03251 integrates substance abuse and psychiatric care under unified regional framework. HD03260 modernises research ethics oversight.
