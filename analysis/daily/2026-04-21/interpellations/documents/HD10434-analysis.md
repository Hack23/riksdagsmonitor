## Significance Score: 7.4 / 10

High salience — Stockholm housing is the single largest regional policy crisis in Sweden, and the interpellation uses Boverket/SCB data showing housing starts running ~900 units below the already-inadequate 2025 baseline. S crystallises this as an indictment of the coalition's housing strategy, specifically targeting Carlson (KD) who has already accumulated multiple interpellations across infrastructure and housing portfolios.

## Summary

The interpellation asks Carlson (a) what concrete measures will accelerate Stockholm housing starts back to the needs-based level (estimated 20 000+/year), (b) how the government reconciles ROT/räntetak/byggbonus tool-kit with continuing underperformance, (c) what it will do about the collapse in multi-family-housing starts (bostadsrätter and hyresrätter), and (d) how social housing and starter-housing shortages are being addressed in the capital region.

## Political Analysis

**Carlson cumulative exposure**: HD10434 is one of several interpellations targeting Carlson (along with HD10428 on airports and earlier housing questions). Cumulative pressure creates a "minister cannot manage portfolio" narrative — which is what S is constructing for the campaign.

**Tool-kit underperformance**: The government has announced ROT expansions, byggbonus, simpler detaljplan-processer, and interest-cap (räntetak) ideas. Starts still fall. Carlson must either explain why the tools are working despite the headline numbers (hard), or concede the tools are insufficient (easy campaign line for S).

**Stockholm-region specificity**: Although headlines are national, the housing shortfall is concentrated in Stockholm. S can credibly argue that the government is not delivering in Sweden's most economically critical region — a direct hit on M and KD's competence claim.

**Demand-side and supply-side linkage**: Housing shortfall affects labour-market mobility (people cannot move to take jobs), tax base (lost wages and returns), and welfare (housing insecurity, homelessness). The interpellation therefore connects to Finance Ministry (Svantesson) and Social Ministry (Lann/Forssmed) frames — magnifying political surface area.

**Youth and newcomer impact**: First-time buyers and renters are the population most exposed. Politically salient in student cities, among young voters, and in integration debates (newcomers to Sweden disproportionately settle in Stockholm).
