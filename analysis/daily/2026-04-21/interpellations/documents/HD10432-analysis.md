## Significance Score: 7.2 / 10

Structural fiscal-healthcare intersection question. Many Swedish hospital buildings are from the 1960s–1970s and are failing simultaneously. Regions finance healthcare buildings through borrowing (Kommuninvest), but interest-rate normalisation since 2022 has doubled or tripled their debt-service costs — exposing a mismatch between the capital programme and the ownership model. S asks whether the state should underwrite or co-finance hospital rebuilds; the answer has multi-billion-krona fiscal consequences.

## Summary

The interpellation to Sjukvårdsminister Lann (KD) asks whether the government will introduce state guarantees, direct co-financing, or fiscal-space accommodation for regions investing in hospital buildings, given that (a) a significant share of Swedish hospital building stock is from the 1960s, (b) regions face simultaneous renewal needs, (c) post-2022 interest-rate environment has made borrowing substantially more expensive, and (d) operational pressure from longer waiting lists coexists with constrained capital budgets.

## Political Analysis

**The fiscal-federalism question**: Sweden's governance model assigns healthcare financing and infrastructure to the regions (21 regions since 2020), with the state supplying statsbidrag (general and targeted grants). A state guarantee of hospital-building investment would partially federalise healthcare capital — a historic shift. S is therefore raising a constitutional-operational question with a budget-office lens.

**KD's dilemma**: KD's brand includes healthcare competence and a preference for strong central coordination of healthcare. Rejecting the interpellation outright conflicts with party identity; accepting it conflicts with Finansdepartementet (Svantesson, M) fiscal orthodoxy. Lann must navigate this carefully.

**Svantesson linkage**: Although Lann is the named addressee, any state-guarantee or capital-injection commitment crosses Svantesson's perimeter. This interpellation is therefore indirectly pressure on the Finance Minister — aligning with HD10442 and HD10433 in creating cumulative Svantesson exposure.

**Waiting-list linkage**: Hospital capital constraints translate into operational bottlenecks (delayed ward reopenings, deferred equipment, operating-room capacity). The government's vårdgaranti narrative is vulnerable when building-stock failure is identified as the upstream cause.

**Comparative precedent**: The UK NHS, Danish Sundhedsstruktur, and Norwegian helseforetak models all rely on national-level financing for hospital buildings. Sweden is an outlier in pure regional self-finance for major hospital projects. S can cite these comparators.
