## Significance Score: 6.5 / 10

C's interpellation deliberately positions the party in the liberal-internationalist tradition it shares with MP/L opposition-side coalitions. It challenges Dousa, who oversees Sweden's development aid (bistånd), to articulate how LGBTQI+ rights have fared in the shift to the Tidö-era development policy emphasising regional focus and narrower thematic priorities.

## Summary

The interpellation asks Dousa (a) how the government's LGBTQI+ rights agenda is reflected in the current bistånd-strategi and Sida-portfolio, (b) whether funding to LGBTQI+ organisations in high-risk countries (Uganda, Russia, Hungary, parts of MENA) has been sustained, increased, or cut, (c) whether Sweden continues to use its voice in UN fora (HRC resolutions, SOGI mandate) and in EU external action, and (d) how bilateral and multilateral channels are balanced given global rights regression.

## Political Analysis

**C's strategic positioning**: Filing this interpellation lets C differentiate from the M/KD/L/SD bloc without breaking from its centre-right economic identity. Human-rights commitments help C retain urban liberal voters and young professionals wavering between C and L.

**Dousa's response dilemma**:
1. Affirm continuity and specify numbers → creates documented accountability baseline C and NGOs will monitor
2. Speak generally → enables opposition to claim the government has downgraded rights priorities
3. Shift to "we deliver through EU/UN rather than bilaterally" → technically defensible but signals reduced direct Swedish agency

**Coalition internal dimension**: KD and SD base constituencies are less aligned with LGBTQI+ rights internationalism than M/L. Dousa (M) must answer in a way that does not provoke internal-coalition friction while also avoiding NGO / media characterisation as retreat.

**Resource-allocation reality**: The 2023–25 bistånd reform narrowed thematic focus and reduced core support to some civil-society actors. Whether LGBTQI+ funding specifically has been sustained is a verifiable question — Sida publishes disaggregated data. Any Dousa answer that diverges from published data risks later fact-checking.

**External ecosystem**: Swedish LGBTQI+ NGOs (RFSL) and their international counterparts treat parliamentary records as campaign-advocacy inputs. The interpellation will generate policy-advocacy uptake regardless of outcome.
