## Full Question Analysis

**The interpellation concerns**:
EU Directive 2023/970 on Pay Transparency (Lönetransparensdirektivet) must be transposed into Swedish law by June 7, 2026. The directive requires:
- Employers with 100+ employees to publish salary statistics
- Prohibition on pay secrecy clauses
- Right for employees to request pay comparison data
- Joint pay assessments where gender pay gap exceeds 5%

**Sweden's current situation**: Despite being a self-declared leader on gender equality, Sweden's gender pay gap has been **persistent and even growing** in recent years. While Sweden performs better than EU average, the interpellation notes that the gap remains and the directive would provide structural mechanisms to address it.

**The question**: When and how will Minister Larsson ensure Sweden's transposition of the EU Pay Transparency Directive?
