## Significance Score: 5.9 / 10

Regional infrastructure question with outsized campaign value in the Dalarna / Härjedalen / Norrbotten arc. S is probing why Trafikverket's evaluation did not result in beredskapsflygplats status for an airport that serves as the only reliable year-round aviation node between Mora/Östersund for parts of the Scandinavian mountain region — a classic rural-vs-central-government frame.

## Summary

The interpellation asks Minister Carlson why Scandinavian Mountain Airport (IATA: SCR, Sälen/Trysil area) was not designated a beredskapsflygplats (emergency/preparedness airport) in the most recent Trafikverket review, whether the minister intends to revise the designation criteria, and what alternative arrangements exist for medical evacuation (HEMS/ambulansflyg), civil-defence logistics, and total-defence (totalförsvar) operations in the Scandinavian mountain region.

## Political Analysis

**Total-defence dimension**: Post-NATO accession (March 2024), Swedish military aviation requires resilient, geographically dispersed runway access. S frames the non-designation as inconsistent with the totalförsvar doctrine that KD/M/L/SD coalition has championed. This is a strong rhetorical card.

**Regional-justice framing**: Rural constituencies perceive Stockholm-centric infrastructure decisions as symptomatic of elite indifference. The interpellation taps this pattern. The Dalarna/Härjedalen vote is politically competitive — C, S, SD and M all compete there.

**Private ownership complication**: The airport is privately operated, which complicates a public designation decision (cost-sharing, state aid under EU rules). Carlson's defensive response likely emphasises this complexity — but that itself becomes a campaign line ("the minister hides behind paperwork while rural Sweden lacks medical air lifts").

**Ambulance-helicopter dependency**: HEMS operations in the mountains are operationally dependent on open airports for refuelling, crew rotation and all-weather diversions. If Scandinavian Mountain Airport loses viability, regions downgrade HEMS coverage — a direct health-outcome consequence.
