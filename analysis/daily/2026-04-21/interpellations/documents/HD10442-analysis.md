## Summary

This is the most politically explosive interpellation of the day. Markus Kallifatides (S) uses a 2026-04-01 Stockholm Tingsrätt court judgment to directly confront Finance Minister Elisabeth Svantesson with evidence that her public statements about eating disorder care were false. Svantesson stated in the Riksdag on 2025-09-22: "Here in Stockholm... they raise taxes and close eating disorder clinics because they can't understand how to prioritize and have a tax policy that benefits hard-working people." The court ruling exposed the reality: Stockholm Region REORGANIZED (not closed) eating disorder care, improving treatment access from 38% (2022, when M ran Stockholm) to 94% (2025), and won a 67 million SEK lawsuit against the private company that lost its contract. Kallifatides asks two questions: Does Svantesson maintain her statement? And if not, what action will she take?

## Political Analysis

**The Trap Structure**: Kallifatides has constructed a perfect parliamentary accountability trap. Svantesson has only two options:
1. **Maintain her statement**: She must defend claims that are now directly contradicted by (a) Stockholm Region's own data showing improved access, (b) a court judgment confirming the private company was acting against Region Stockholm's interests, and (c) evidence that the reorganization — not "closure" — was the policy
2. **Retract her statement**: She must publicly acknowledge making misleading statements in the Riksdag — an extremely rare and politically costly admission for a Finance Minister 18 months before an election

**The Finance Minister's Unusual Role**: It is unusual for a Finance Minister to make specific claims about regional healthcare reorganizations. Svantesson's statement appears to have been made as part of an election-year attack on the Social Democrat-led Stockholm Region using healthcare as a political weapon. The court judgment exposes this tactic.

**The 94% vs 38% Contrast**: The most devastating fact in this interpellation is the before-and-after access rate. During Moderaterna's last years running Stockholm Region (2022), only 38% of patients received timely eating disorder treatment per the care guarantee. Under the new organization (2025), 94% do. This directly inverts Svantesson's narrative: the "closure" she denounced was actually an improvement.

**The 67 Million SEK Judgment**: The court ruling establishing that the private company owed Stockholm Region 67 million SEK contextualizes why the company lost its contract — it was not a politically motivated "closure" but a governance decision that the courts have now validated.

**Election Implications**: Svantesson is one of Sweden's most prominent M politicians and the government's chief economic voice. A parliamentary record of making false healthcare statements in the Riksdag, which a court has subsequently discredited, is precisely the kind of documented accountability failure that S opposition research teams will deploy in the September 2026 election campaign.
