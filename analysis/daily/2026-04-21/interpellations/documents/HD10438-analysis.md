## Full Question Analysis

**The interpellation concerns**:
Civil society organizations (ideburna organisationer) providing women's shelter services for domestic violence survivors are closing across Sweden. The shelters play a fundamental role in Sweden's anti-violence against women strategy — they are the emergency safety net for women fleeing abusive relationships.

**What's driving closures**: Changes in municipal funding models, competitive tendering processes that favor large commercial providers over specialist NGOs, and funding pressures on the civil society sector are cited as drivers. The ideburet sector argues that commercial operators cannot replicate the specific trauma-informed, peer-support model that specialist women's organizations provide.

**Key question posed**: What does the minister intend to do to ensure that women's shelters can continue to operate across the country?

## Political Intelligence Assessment

**Moral urgency**: Unlike most interpellations, this directly involves personal safety of vulnerable individuals. The closure of a women's shelter can be directly linked to risk of violence against survivors who have nowhere to turn. This creates high media transmissibility and emotional resonance.

**Election 2026**: Sweden's record on violence against women and gender equality is considered a core national value. Any suggestion that government policy has contributed to the collapse of the safety net for DV survivors will resonate strongly.
