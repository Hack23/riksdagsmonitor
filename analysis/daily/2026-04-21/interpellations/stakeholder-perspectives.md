**Evidence Table**:

|-------|--------|-------------------|--------|
| Stockholm police gap (~1,000 officers missing) | Direct safety risk | ~2.4M Stockholm residents, esp. in vulnerable areas | frs 2025/26:439; BRÅ March 2026 |

These are the issues that drive voter behavior, particularly among women (shelter closures + wage gap) and urban residents (Stockholm police).

**Key Business Issues**:

### 5. Civil Society Organizations

**Perspective**: Existential — women's shelter funding model is under direct threat

**Evidence**:
- Women's shelters (ideburna organisationer) are closing at accelerating rate since government funding changes
- Organizations explicitly name the gap between government rhetoric on domestic violence prevention and funding reality
- Civil society model for delivering social services is under systemic pressure in this parliament

**Civil Society Perspective** [HIGH confidence]: This is not a marginal issue — it directly affects whether the NGO sector can continue to provide violence prevention services. The interpellation from Sofia Amloh is an explicit advocacy act on behalf of the ideburet sector.

### 6. International and EU Community

**Perspective**: Rule of law compliance and bilateral relations

**EU Dimension** [HIGH confidence]: The European Commission is monitoring Pay Directive transposition across all member states. Sweden's 47-day compliance window is now at risk of failure, which would result in formal EU infringement proceedings — embarrassing for a country that positions itself as an EU law compliance leader.

**Israel/Palestine Dimension** [MEDIUM confidence]: The Bernadotte interpellation (HD10435) and Israel death penalty laws interpellation (HD10426) both require Sweden to take positions on Israel's conduct. Given ongoing Gaza ceasefire diplomacy, any statement by the Foreign Minister will be scrutinized by multiple international actors.
