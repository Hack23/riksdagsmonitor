## Overall Threat Assessment

The April 21, 2026 interpellation landscape represents an **escalating parliamentary accountability offensive** by the Social Democrats (S) against the Tidö coalition government, coordinated to maximize pre-election damage across five policy domains simultaneously. With 439 interpellations filed in 2025/26, parliamentary scrutiny has intensified dramatically as the September 2026 election approaches.

**Severity: HIGH** — Coalition faces simultaneous EU legal compliance deadline, domestic violence infrastructure crisis, police deployment controversy, and infrastructure accountability burden, all within 45 days before summer parliamentary recess.

## Threat 1: EU Pay Transparency Directive Non-Compliance (CRITICAL)

**Nature**: Legal/Political hybrid threat  
**Actors**: European Commission, S (Sofia Amloh), Nina Larsson (L)  
**Timeline**: June 7, 2026 (47 days)  
**dok_id**: HD10437 (frs 2025/26:437)

Sweden must transpose EU Directive 2023/970 on Pay Transparency by June 7, 2026. The directive requires employers with 100+ employees to provide salary transparency data and prohibits pay secrecy. Sweden's gender pay gap has been **persistent and growing** — contrary to Sweden's self-image as a gender equality leader. The opposition interpellation forces the minister to either:
- Commit to rapid transposition (validating opposition's urgency framing), OR  
- Defend delay (risking EU infringement and election narrative "Government fails women")

**[HIGH] Confidence**: EU infringement deadline is statutory and non-negotiable.

## Threat 2: Women's Shelter Closures (HIGH)

**Nature**: Social infrastructure crisis / Election narrative  
**Actors**: S (Sofia Amloh), Nina Larsson (L), ideburet sector  
**Timeline**: Ongoing, response deadline 2026-05-05  
**dok_id**: HD10438 (frs 2025/26:438)

Women's shelters (kvinnojourer) operated by civil society organizations are closing across Sweden due to funding pressures. This creates a double threat: immediate risk to domestic violence survivors' safety, and a powerful election narrative that the government has allowed civil society violence prevention infrastructure to collapse. The interpellation was filed on the same day as HD10437, suggesting coordinated strategic intent by S to overwhelm Nina Larsson and capture a "gender equality minister failing women" headline.

**[HIGH] Confidence**: Sector closure data is documented by ideburet organizations.

## Threat 3: Stockholm Police Deficit (HIGH)

**Nature**: Policy credibility threat  
**Actors**: S (Mattias Vepsä), Gunnar Strömmer (M), BRÅ  
**Timeline**: Response deadline 2026-04-30  
**dok_id**: HD10439 (frs 2025/26:439)

The government's signature policing achievement — 10,000 new police officers — is undermined by BRÅ's own evaluation finding that Stockholm is the **only region where police density is declining** relative to population growth. With ~1,000 officers missing relative to other regions, and Stockholm hosting 25% of Sweden's population, 50% of vulnerable housing areas, parliament, embassies, and major events, the interpellation creates a "goals met, capital unsafe" narrative.

**[HIGH] Confidence**: BRÅ data is authoritative and non-partisan.

## Threat 4: Andreas Carlson Accountability Accumulation (MEDIUM-HIGH)

**Nature**: Ministerial overload / Narrative consolidation risk  
**Actors**: S (multiple MPs), Andreas Carlson (KD)  
**Timeline**: Ongoing — 9 active interpellations  
**dok_ids**: HD10434, HD10428, HD10425, HD10424, HD10418, HD10417, HD10413, HD10412, HD10410

No minister in the current session faces as many interpellations as Andreas Carlson (KD), responsible for both Infrastructure and Housing. The subjects span rail failures (Västerdalsbanan), road safety (Riksväg 62), housing starts decline, airport closures, defense infrastructure costs, and housing accessibility — creating a "ministry in chaos" narrative if not actively countered. Individual responses may be adequate; the accumulation pattern is the strategic threat.

**[MEDIUM] Confidence**: Individual policy responses available, but pattern is documented.

## Threat 5: Foreign Policy Exposure — Israel/Bernadotte (MEDIUM)

**Nature**: Diplomatic/Historical accountability  
**Actor**: Jamal El-Haj (-), Maria Malmer Stenergard (M)  
**Timeline**: Response deadline 2026-04-30
