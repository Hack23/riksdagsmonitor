## Executive Summary

PM Ulf Kristersson and Minister Erik Slottner (Finansdepartementet) submit Prop 2025/26:244, establishing new interoperability requirements for data sharing across public administration. This digital governance reform mandates standardized data exchange between government agencies, municipalities, and regions — a foundational measure for Sweden's digital government ambitions. The proposition targets inefficiencies in cross-agency data flows that have hampered public service delivery and policy implementation. Referred to Trafikutskottet (TU), the bill will be debated alongside digital infrastructure issues. **[HIGH confidence]**
