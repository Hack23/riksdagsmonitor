1. "Risk of absconding" with lower burden of proof (from "significant risk" to "assessed risk")

Props 265 covers administrative migration detention — different legal regime, same ECHR Art. 5 scrutiny. Together, these two legislative tracks represent a systematic expansion of Swedish administrative detention powers in a single parliamentary session — unprecedented in scope.

This is a significant sovereignty question: the current law requires government case-by-case decision; prop. 254 grants standing government authority.

**Strategic significance:** With Sweden's NATO membership, this is the "new normal" baseline — all Nordic countries have similar frameworks. But Sweden's 200-year tradition of military non-alignment means this is culturally significant even if legally routine for NATO members.
