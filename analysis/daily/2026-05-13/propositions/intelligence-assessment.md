## Analytic Line

The three propositions together represent the Tidöavtalet government's final legislative push in the 2025/26 riksmöte. The timing — May 2026 submission, September 2026 election — is not accidental. Each proposition advances a different dimension of the Tidöavtalet agenda:

- **HD03267**: Security/migration dimension (SD core policy; M law-and-order)
- **HD03250**: Digital modernisation/state capacity (M governance agenda)
- **HD03261**: Administrative efficiency/benefit system integrity (M+SD welfare chauvinist overlap)

The intelligence value of this batch is primarily in HD03267 as a predictor of post-election governance norms: regardless of whether M+KD+L+SD or an alternative coalition governs after September 2026, the expanded detention powers will be on the statute book.

## Pass 2 Enhancement: Refined Key Judgements

### KJ-1 Refinement: The Legislative Lock-In Strategy

The 1 March 2027 entry-into-force date is not administrative convenience — it is a deliberate legislative strategy. Swedish law makes it procedurally difficult to repeal a law within months of its entry into force: new legislative proposals require ministry drafting, Lagrådet consultation, remiss process, and committee consideration. Minimum timeline from a new government's formation (October 2026) to actual repeal would be 12–18 months. This means:

- Even if an alternative coalition governs from October 2026
- Even if they immediately initiate an amendment process
- HD03267's expanded powers will be operational for at least 12 months before any reversal takes effect

This is the strongest evidence that the proposition is not primarily about the September 2026 election — it is about shaping the governance landscape beyond it.

### KJ-3 Refinement: ECHR Challenge Mechanism

A challenge would likely be filed by either:
(a) A detainee's legal counsel under Article 34 (individual application)
(b) A Nordic human rights NGO supporting an individual application
(c) Potentially the Swedish Bar Association or Amnesty International Sweden

The timeline:
- Entry into force: March 2027
- First detention without time limit: By mid-2027
- Legal challenge filed: 2027–2028
- ECtHR admissibility: 2029–2031
- ECtHR judgment: 2031–2035

This means the ECHR check on this legislation will not materialise until approximately 5–9 years after it is passed. In that window, the expanded powers will have been exercised in an unknown number of cases.

### New KJ-6: Political Cover vs. Legal Risk Trade-off

The government is consciously accepting legal risk (ECHR vulnerability) for political benefit (security/migration narrative in election year). This is rational from a short-term electoral perspective because:
- The ECHR judgment is decades away
- The political benefit is immediate
- No current Swedish government has faced electoral punishment for previous ECHR violations on security matters
- Swedish voters prioritise domestic security concerns over ECtHR compliance in survey data
