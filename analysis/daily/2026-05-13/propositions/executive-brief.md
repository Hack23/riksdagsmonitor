## The Three Propositions

### 1. HD03267 — Stärkt skydd mot utlänningar som utgör kvalificerade säkerhetshot ⚡ HIGH SIGNIFICANCE
**Prop. 2025/26:267 | JuU | Justitiedepartementet | Minister: Gunnar Strömmer**

*What*: Amendments to lagen (2022:700) om särskild kontroll av vissa utlänningar (the "SÄPO law"). Lowers the evidentiary threshold for detention, removes time limits for adult detention, extends detention for children, clarifies expulsion grounds, and increases penalties.  
*Significance*: 🔴 Critical. Expands executive coercive power in a fundamental-rights-sensitive area. Lagrådet consulted (Bilaga 5 present). Election proximity multiplier applies.  
*Entry into force*: 1 March 2027  

### 2. HD03250 — En statlig e-legitimation ⚠️ MEDIUM-HIGH SIGNIFICANCE  
**Prop. 2025/26:250 | TU | Finansdepartementet**

*What*: Establishes a state-issued electronic identity credential. Creates new infrastructure for digital public services, enabling citizens to authenticate with government agencies via a state-controlled e-ID.  
*Significance*: 🟡 Significant. Major digital infrastructure project with implications for national sovereignty over identity systems, competition with BankID (private sector), and GDPR/privacy considerations.

### 3. HD03261 — Utökade befogenheter för Skatteverket inom folkbokföringsverksamheten ⚠️ MEDIUM SIGNIFICANCE
**Prop. 2025/26:261 | SkU | Finansdepartementet**

*What*: Expands Skatteverket's powers in population registration (folkbokföring), likely including data access, verification powers, or enforcement authority.  
*Significance*: 🟡 Significant. Administrative expansion of state monitoring capacity. Statskontoret trigger: cross-agency efficiency implications.

## Pass 2 Enhancement: Specific Evidence

### On HD03267 — Why This Matters NOW

The specific novelty in HD03267 is not merely tighter security — it is the **combination** of three simultaneous changes:
1. Lower evidentiary bar (easier to trigger)
2. No time limit (indefinite once triggered)
3. Children affected (family unit detained)

Each change alone would be contestable. Together, they create a detention regime with no parallel in current Swedish law. The legal architecture is: (1) SÄPO classifies someone as "qualified security threat" → (2) Detention initiated on lower evidence → (3) No mandatory periodic judicial review of time limits → (4) Children of detained adult also at risk of extended detention.

**The ECHR line**: *Chahal v. UK* (1996): Even national security cases require individual rights protection. *J.N. v. UK* (2016): Duration without prospect of removal violates Art. 5. Sweden's proposition creates exactly this duration scenario.

### On HD03250 — The BankID Competition Reality

BankID processes approximately 150 million authentications per month in Sweden. Every major bank (Handelsbanken, SEB, Nordea, Swedbank) is a BankID issuer. The state e-ID will need to achieve comparable adoption to be meaningful — which means either mandating it for government services (which would accelerate adoption) or offering it as an opt-in (which risks creating a two-tier identity system where BankID remains dominant in private services and state e-ID is a niche public-sector solution).

The proposition's committee assignment to TU (Trafikutskottet) rather than KU (Konstitutionsutskottet) or NU suggests it is framed as a transport/digital infrastructure matter rather than a fundamental governance issue — which may affect the depth of constitutional review in committee.

### On HD03261 — The Incremental Accumulation Problem

In the 2021/22 riksmöte, Skatteverket received expanded powers to contact landlords for address verification. In 2022, expanded powers to cooperate with Försäkringskassan on benefit integrity. Now in 2026, HD03261 adds another layer. No single proposition is dramatic. The cumulative access profile of Skatteverket — personal number, address, civil status, benefit claims, tax data, now enhanced registration verification — constitutes an extraordinarily comprehensive data profile of every Swedish resident. There is no Riksdagsmonitor equivalent that audits the cumulative data access of a single agency across multiple riksmöten.
