## FORMATION PREVIEW

The next Swedish government will be formed in the 3-6 weeks after 2026-09-13. The decisive question is not "who wins" but "what SD demands" and "what C decides."

**Three opening moves that will define the 2026-2030 mandate**:

1. **SD Cabinet Entry or Exclusion**: If Tidö wins and Åkesson demands a ministerial portfolio, Sweden's constitutional debate will define the government's first 90 days. This decision (YES or NO to SD in cabinet) will be the most consequential Swedish political choice since EU accession (1994).

2. **Nuclear Construction Decision**: HD01NU19 has created the legal pathway. The next government must announce its nuclear decision within Year 1 (T+365d). Electricity demand doubling by 2040 means this cannot be deferred forever. The next mandate's energy security record will be defined by this single choice.

3. **Housing Emergency Programme**: The 200,000+ unit deficit is a structural crisis. Any government that fails to address this will face an electorate in 2030 that has experienced 8+ years of worsening housing affordability. Sweden needs 25,000+ new housing starts per year; 2026-2030 must reverse the trend.
