## Key Intelligence Summary

**HD03271 "En förändrad abortlag"** is the dominant story. The proposition modernises the 1974 Abortion Act with three major access expansions: (1) home abortions enabled, (2) midwives gain independent authority to conduct medical abortions, (3) telemedicine consultations normalised. The reform enters force 2027-01-01. Lagrådet issued a positive yttrande 2026-04-15 — constitutionally cleared. The reform has cross-party support from M, C, L, S, V, MP but faces SD opposition (values conservatism) and KD internal tension (Health Minister Forssmed submitted the proposition despite KD's traditional-values base).

The **critical intelligence dynamic** is the KD paradox: Forssmed's ministerial submission of an abortion access expansion represents one of the most striking examples of coalition discipline overriding party values in modern Swedish politics. KD's 19 seats hover just above the 4% threshold — any grassroots defection accelerated by this reform could threaten KD's parliamentary survival.

**HD03270** is technical EU compliance (CLP chemicals, waste transport, packaging regulations). Low political salience.
