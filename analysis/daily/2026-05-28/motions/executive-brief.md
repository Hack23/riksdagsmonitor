6. **Government will prevail on votes**: Both propositions will pass before the summer recess. Opposition motions function as election manifesto markers, not blocking instruments.

## Pass 2 Additional Intelligence (2026-05-28)

**Specific textual evidence extracted from full-text review:**

### Prop 267 — Key legal mechanism (from HD024188)
The government's new deportation criterion: *"om det är särskilt påkallat med hänsyn till Sveriges säkerhet"* (if especially warranted with regard to Sweden's security). This replaces the prior standard of "may be assumed to commit terrorist acts." The shift from an individuated threat assessment to a broader security-interest standard is what CRD and Advokatsamfundet object to most specifically.

**V's framing**: LSU is already "synnerligen repressiv och omfattande" (extraordinarily repressive and comprehensive). The new proposal uses "security environment" as justification without specifying what changed — the same technique used for each prior expansion cycle.

### Prop 261 — Key structural concern (from HD024191)
MP's most precise concern: persons who are physically resident in Sweden but **lack a stable bostad (fixed address)** and therefore cannot register for a folkbokföringsadress face a "moment 22" — they are residents but administratively non-existent, losing access to healthcare, social services, and all other address-linked rights. This is not a theoretical risk — an estimated 34,000+ people in Sweden experience homelessness in a given year.

**Electoral significance**: This MP argument has crossover appeal to S voters concerned about social safety nets — potentially enabling S to endorse this specific demand even while supporting the broader prop.
