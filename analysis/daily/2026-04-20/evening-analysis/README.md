## Lead Story At-A-Glance

> **2026 is a "grundlagsval."** Sweden's September 13, 2026 general election will determine not just the government, but the fate of two fundamental law amendments (KU33: police seizure secrecy; KU32: media accessibility) that have passed first reading and await second reading by the post-election Riksdag. This is the first election in modern Swedish history where voters are simultaneously choosing constitutional principles. The Kristersson government launched a comprehensive 9-proposition pre-election package (led by Spring Economic Bill HD03100) while the opposition responded with an unprecedented 21 coordinated counter-motions and 10 targeted interpellations. 146 days remain until the most consequential Swedish election since at least 1994.
