## 2. Core Analysis

### What the Proposal Does
Sweden's "god man" (good man) and "förvaltare" (administrator/trustee) system supports approximately 100,000 adults who lack full legal capacity due to illness, disability, or age. The reform:

1. **Clearer mandate frameworks** — the legal representative's tasks become more precisely defined
2. **Individual autonomy primacy** — increased respect for the wishes and wellbeing of the person being represented (stronger CRPD alignment)
3. **Central oversight authority** — a new state body to monitor, train, and certify legal representatives (replacing fragmented municipal oversight)

### Why Zero Reservations?
Remarkable political consensus. The previous system had known problems for over a decade:
- Social workers and volunteers (godmän) serving as legal reps with insufficient oversight
- Cases of financial exploitation and mismanagement
- Municipal court oversight (tingsrätt + overförmyndarnämnd) fragmented and underfunded

A 2021 government investigation (SOU) mapped systemic failures. All parties supported the reform framework.

### Scale of Impact
- ~100,000 persons currently under some form of legal representation in Sweden
- ~50,000 volunteer "godmän" serving as legal representatives
- Approximately 50,000 cases of *god manskapets* estimated to end or restructure under new rules
