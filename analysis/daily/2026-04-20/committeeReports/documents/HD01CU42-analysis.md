## 2. Core Analysis

### What the Proposal Does
The Swedish National Audit Office (Riksrevisionen) audited how state agencies handle estates (*dödsbon*) — particularly the Enforcement Authority (*Kronofogden*) and local authorities when no heirs are available or when estates are insolvent. CU42 is the parliamentary committee's handling of the government's written response to the Riksrevisionen report.

**Key Riksrevisionen Findings:**
1. Current estate handling rules are fragmented across multiple regulatory frameworks
2. No single authority has comprehensive responsibility
3. Insolvent estates (where debts exceed assets) are handled inefficiently
4. Digital communication possibilities not fully utilised

**CU Position:** Supports the Riksrevisionen recommendations in principle but no immediate legislative proposal — government tasked to investigate further.
