## Behavioural Persuasion Analysis

Applying political psychology (see `behavioral-analysis` skill):

1. **Loss aversion (HD03236)**: Presenting fuel cost as "restored" after years of increase triggers loss-aversion re-set — more salient than absolute benefit.
2. **Identity protection (HD03237)**: Paid police training reinforces law-and-order identity for SD-aligned voters. Counter-intuitive insight: it **does not** strongly persuade undecided voters; it mobilises existing supporters.
3. **Status-quo bias (HD03238)**: Environmental agency change triggers negative status-quo reactions from MP+V voters disproportionately; loss-framed by opposition.
4. **Bandwagon (HD03220)**: NATO deployment enjoys consensus, making dissent feel marginal; stabilising effect across cross-bloc.
5. **Temporal discounting**: Fuel cut visible in weeks; environmental agency benefits years out. Political science shows near-term visible benefits outweigh larger future benefits in voter perception by 3–5x.

## Turnout Differential Risk

- **Government-favourable turnout boost**: Segments 1+2+7 (rural drivers, suburban homeowners, small business) — fuel cut + police reform. Est. +0.5–1.0 pp bloc advantage via differential turnout.
- **Opposition-favourable turnout boost**: Segments 3+4+8 (urban white-collar, LO working-class, public sector) — welfare framing, GDP gap. Est. +0.7–1.3 pp bloc advantage.

These largely cancel. Turnout is unlikely to be decisively tilted by the package alone.

## Strategic Implications

1. **Segment 2 (suburban homeowners) is the decisive prize.** Both HD03237 and HD03236 are explicitly designed for this segment. Opposition counter-strategy must neutralise police-competence narrative while amplifying welfare-funding concern.
2. **Segment 1 (rural drivers) will vote bloc reliably**; HD03236 is maintenance, not expansion.
3. **Segment 6 (young voters) and segment 8 (public sector)** are where opposition sees mobilisation upside.
4. **Liberals (L) 4%-threshold risk** in Stockholm professional class: HD03238 environmental agency controversy could cost L its seat block, triggering coalition redefinition.
5. **Swing constituencies in Södermanland, Östergötland, Kronoberg, Skåne S** are the tightest battlegrounds — these are exactly where fuel tax (HD03236) and police expansion (HD03237) are most salient.
