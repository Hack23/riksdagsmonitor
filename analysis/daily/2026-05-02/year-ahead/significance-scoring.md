## Justifications for Top Scores

### HD03262 — Abolition of Permanent Residence Permits (6.0)
This single legislation reshapes Sweden's signal as a destination country for international protection applicants and economic migrants. It directly challenges EU long-term resident directive obligations and ECHR Art. 8 protection scope. Its passage before September 2026 is uncertain (Lagrådet referral risk), but its political significance as a campaign signal for both Tidö and opposition is maximal. Electoral salience: 9/10. Coalition impact: +2 for SD, -1 for KD humanitarian wing, neutral L (abstain risk). 

### HC01FiU20 — Spring Fiscal Bill 2026 (6.0)
Locks fiscal framework for the election year, including the US tariff shock downgrade to 1.2% growth. Determines headroom for all parties' pre-election spending commitments. The document directly anchors the economic narrative for the 2026 campaign. Economic scope: 10/10. Electoral relevance: 10/10.

### HD10448 — SD–KD Energy Interpellation (5.25)
Represents the clearest articulation of the coalition's unresolved structural fault line. Not a legislative document per se, but functions as a forcing function on the budget and energy framework negotiations. Coalition impact: highest of any document in the cycle.
