### KJ-1 Enhancement (CU31 Legal Challenge)
**Legal basis for challenge**: Hyresgästföreningen has three potential challenge vectors: (1) **EU Services Directive** — market rent introduction may require EU notification; (2) **Swedish constitutional review** (KU/Lagrådet) — if implementation regulations are issued by government rather than Riksdag, constitutional challenge possible; (3) **ECHR Protocol 1, Art.1** (property rights) — paradoxically, landlords challenging if transitional rules limit rent increases too sharply. The most likely challenge path is the EU Services Directive route, which Hyresgästföreningen's legal team can file with Commission within 3 months of enactment.

### KJ-3 Enhancement (Malmer Stenergard/Gaza)
**Historical calibration**: In Nov 2023, Malmer Stenergard said Sweden "calls on" Israel to respect international humanitarian law — the weakest formulation used by a Nordic country. In Feb 2024, following Rafah offensive, Sweden "strongly urges" (med kraft uppmanar) restraint — a marginal escalation. The escalation pattern requires a specific Swedish-citizen incident to advance further. The written question (HD11803) from Büser does not allege injury — it asks about the interception of a vessel carrying Swedish citizens on international waters. This is factually established (the flotilla was interdicted). The legal question is whether international water interdiction without injury/detention triggers a mandatory Swedish diplomatic response. **Answer**: Not mandatory under Vienna Convention (which only covers nationals in foreign territory) — but politically expected given Nordic comparators.
