## Core Intelligence

**Motion type**: Kommittémotion  
**Riksdag demand**: C seeks threshold amendment — deportation should require "systematic repeated offenses over time" rather than the government's proposed lower threshold

**Political significance**: This is the strategically highest-leverage motion in the entire April batch. C is a government-adjacent party in the Tidö arrangement; its filing of a substantive amendment to a core migration bill is a direct challenge to SD's legislative priorities and signals C's ongoing positioning as a centrist brake on the coalition's rightward migration trajectory.

**Key arguments** (per summary):
- C argues the proposed deportation criteria are too broad and would capture non-serious offenders
- C's demand — "systematiska upprepade fall av brott över tid" (systematic repeated offenses over time) — is a higher evidentiary threshold than the government proposes
- C is not opposing the principle of deportation for criminal conduct — it supports tougher responses to serious crime — but seeks a legally sound threshold

**Consequence analysis**: If C votes for its own amendment in SfU, it splits the Tidö arithmetic. The government must then either accept C's threshold (weakening the bill's punitive reach) or outvote C (publicly humiliating a coalition partner). Either outcome is politically costly.

**Election 2026 significance**: C's threshold motion is a public signal to centrist voters that C has not abandoned rule-of-law principles despite coalition membership with SD. This is likely Paarup-Petersen's intent — create a visible, recorded disagreement on migration hardening.
