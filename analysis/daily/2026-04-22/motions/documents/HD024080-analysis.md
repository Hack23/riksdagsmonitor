## Core Intelligence

**Key arguments** (per summary): S demands that mottagningscenter not be transferable to private management — ensuring public control of asylum accommodation. This is a public-sector ownership principle applied to migration reception context.

**Consequence analysis**: S's limited-scope motion on mottagandelagen reflects its electoral positioning: not opposing the reception law framework entirely, but defending public-sector control as a core principle. This allows S to appear tough on managing migration while opposing privatisation — two priorities for S's voter coalition.
