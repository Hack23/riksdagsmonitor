## Core Intelligence

**Motion type**: Kommittémotion (committee motion — indicates coordinated party position, not individual)  
**Riksdag demand**: S demands the government return to Riksdag with a holistic climate-fiscal framework; explicitly opposes the fuel tax cut in prop. 2025/26:236

**Political significance**: This is the Social Democrats' formal entry into the fiscal-climate battleground of spring 2026. By filing a committee motion rather than an individual motion, S signals this is an official party position — making it a pre-election commitment visible in the voting record.

**Key arguments** (per summary):
- The government's fuel tax cut is fiscally inconsistent with Sweden's climate obligations
- S demands a broader policy framework be brought back to Riksdag
- The combined electricity/gas price support (the other element of prop. 2025/26:236) is not challenged — S's opposition is specifically to the drivmedelsskattesänkning

**Consequence analysis**: S voting against the government on a fiscal matter is standard procedure, but the framing here — "return to Riksdag with a plan" rather than "reject entirely" — suggests S is positioning for a constructive opposition image, not just obstructionism. This allows S to say in the 2026 campaign: "We offered a better way; the government refused."
