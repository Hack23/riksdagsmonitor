**Riksdag demand**: V rejects the law amendment to 1992 arms export legislation; rejects the government's proposal on follow-on deliveries

**Key arguments** (per summary):

- References the 1992 legislation (lagen om krigsmateriel, 1992:1300) — indicating V seeks to retain the existing stricter framework rather than adopt the "modernised" version
- V's position: the new framework would loosen arms export criteria in ways inconsistent with Sweden's humanitarian obligations

**Consequence analysis**: This motion will almost certainly be rejected in UU given the pro-NATO majority. However, V's specific citation of the 1992 legislation creates a legal paper trail relevant to future export licensing decisions.
