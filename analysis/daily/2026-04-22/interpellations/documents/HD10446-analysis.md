## 🔬 Document Content Summary

Åsa Eriksson (S) raises the issue of incorrect death declarations (felaktiga dödförklaringar) by Skatteverket — approximately 30 people per year are mistakenly declared dead in Sweden's civil register. The interpellation cites a response Finance Minister Svantesson gave to a written parliamentary question (skriftlig fråga), in which Svantesson acknowledged the ~30 cases/year and stated Skatteverket notifies relevant actors immediately.

Eriksson contests the effectiveness of this, citing a case from Västmanland where the process failed. She asks whether the minister intends to take action to: (a) reduce the occurrence of false death declarations, and (b) better support those who are incorrectly declared dead in restoring their records.
