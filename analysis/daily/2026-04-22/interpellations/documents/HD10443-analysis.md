## 🔬 Document Content Summary

Peder Björk (S) challenges Civil Minister Erik Slottner (KD) on the continuing practice of "social dumping" — municipalities informally pressuring vulnerable people (those on social assistance, needing housing support) to relocate to other municipalities, often to weaker labour markets, lower-quality housing, and fewer social services.

The interpellation references:
- The previous Social Democratic government's investigation mandate to combat social dumping
- Media reports of coerced relocations to substandard housing
- The Tidö government's failure to act on this mandate

**Key question**: Does the minister recognise social dumping between municipalities as a serious social problem, and if so, what actions will be taken?

**Related document**: HD10423 (Eva Lindh, S, 2026-03-30 "Åtgärder mot social dumpning") — same minister, same topic — already has a scheduled reply date of 2026-05-05. Both will likely receive a joint answer.

### Policy Background
Social dumping is a structural problem in the Swedish municipal welfare model. Wealthy municipalities "export" social cases to poorer municipalities. The previous S government launched an investigation (dir. cited). The Tidö coalition has not progressed this.
