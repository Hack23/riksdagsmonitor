## 🔬 Document Content Summary

Markus Kallifatides (S) challenges Finance Minister Elisabeth Svantesson (M) over repeated false statements about Region Stockholm's eating disorder care. The interpellation:

1. Documents Svantesson's September 22, 2025 parliamentary statement claiming Region Stockholm "dismantled eating disorder clinics because it cannot understand how to prioritise and have a tax policy that benefits hard-working people"
2. Notes that Social Minister Forssmed (KD) at an interpellation debate on October 24, 2025 could not produce any evidence basis for the Finance Minister's claims
3. Documents Region Stockholm's restructuring of eating disorder care to concentrate at Stockholms centrum för ätstörningar (SCÄ), improving treatment accessibility from **38% (2022, under Moderate governance) to 94% (2025)**
4. Cites **Stockholm District Court ruling of April 1, 2026**: Region Stockholm's claim against a former care provider upheld in full — the company must repay approximately **67 million SEK** (plus litigation costs) for unjustified billing

**Questions posed**:
1. Does the minister maintain her claim that Region Stockholm acted because it "cannot understand how to prioritise"?
2. If not, does the minister intend to take any actions within her portfolio regarding affected patients, relatives, staff, civil servants, and politicians in Region Stockholm?
