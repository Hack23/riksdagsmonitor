## 🔬 Document Content Summary

Jonathan Svensson (S) challenges Finance Minister Elisabeth Svantesson (M) over the government's employer contributions (arbetsgivaravgift) reduction of 10.9% for young workers, effective April 2026. The interpellation cites an Aftonbladet investigative series ("200 sekunder") showing a major retailer directing store managers to redirect the savings into profits rather than job creation — explicitly contrary to the government's stated purpose.

**Key questions posed**:
1. How does the minister view the outcome when tax relief intended for youth employment instead finances company profits?
2. Will the minister take action to ensure the employer contribution reduction achieves its intended effect?
