# Document Analysis: HD0399

**Title**: Vårändringsbudget för 2026  
**Type**: prop

**Date**: 2026-04-13  
**Minister**: Elisabeth Svantesson (M)  
**Department**: Finansdepartementet  
**Source**: riksdagen.se/HD0399  
**Analyst**: James Pether Sörling  

## Analysis

The 2026 Spring Amendment Budget (2025/26:99) implements in-year corrections to the approved 2026 budget. Key adjustments:

- **Appropriation changes**: Corrections to spending forecasts based on actual Q1 2026 outcomes
- **New spending authorisations**: Funding for HD03237 paid police training programme
- **Revenue corrections**: Updates reflecting latest tax receipt projections
- **Alignment with vårpropositionen**: HD0399 is the technical implementation of HD03100's framework

**Why it matters**: The vårändringsbudget is the legal instrument that makes budget changes operational. Without it, agencies cannot receive adjusted appropriations. The inclusion of police training funding is a political signal of priority.
