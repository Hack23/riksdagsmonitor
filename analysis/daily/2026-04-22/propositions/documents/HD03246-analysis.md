## Analysis

Stricter Rules for Young Offenders (2025/26:246) tightens the juvenile justice framework:

- **Expanded detention conditions**: Expands conditions under which under-18 year olds can be held in secure youth care (LVU) following criminal conviction
- **Recidivist sentencing**: Harsher sentencing for youth who reoffend within 2 years
- **Electronic monitoring**: Expanded use of ankle tags for youth on probation
- **Gang membership presumption**: New rules creating a presumption regarding gang affiliation for repeat offenders

**Why it matters**: Youth gang violence (particularly in Stockholm, Malmö, Gothenburg) has been a dominant political issue since 2022. This proposition is the latest in the Tidöavtalets crime reform package. The government argues firm early intervention breaks gang recruitment cycles.

**Political dynamics**: 
- SD wants more (adult prosecution of 15+)
- Opposition argues prevention underfunded relative to punishment
- Human rights organisations (Barnombudsmannen) likely to criticise expanded detention
