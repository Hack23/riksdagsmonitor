## Analysis

National Strategy Against Violence Against Women (2025/26:245):

- **Comprehensive framework**: Updated national strategy against men's violence against women, intimate partner violence, prostitution, human trafficking, and honour-based violence
- **20-agency coordination**: Binds 20 government agencies to implement coordinated response
- **Prevention**: New emphasis on primary prevention (addressing attitudes and norms) alongside perpetrator intervention
- **Support services**: Increased funding for shelters (skyddat boende) and victim support services
- **Honour violence**: Specific provisions for communities where honour-based violence and restrictions are prevalent

**Why it matters**: Sweden has a long history as a leader in gender equality, but violence against women statistics have not improved proportionally. The strategy represents the Tidö coalition's approach — combining conservative criminal justice measures with (L's) liberal emphasis on individual freedom from family control.

**Political dynamics**: This is one of the more cross-party areas — S, C, L, M, and even V support strong action. SD has been more ambiguous on honour violence specifically (in conflict with immigration narrative).
