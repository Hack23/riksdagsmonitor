## Analysis

Sweden's accession to the Council of Europe Register of Damages for Ukraine (2025/26:232):

- **Register of Damages**: International mechanism to document and compensate losses from Russian aggression against Ukraine
- **Swedish contribution**: Sweden commits to support the compensation mechanism and provide funding
- **Legal basis**: Council of Europe framework; 46+ states participating
- **No direct financial liability**: Sweden does not assume individual claims — funds the mechanism's operations

**Why it matters**: This proposition is part of Sweden's broader Ukraine accountability agenda. Combined with HD03231 (aggression tribunal), Sweden is positioning itself as a leading advocate for the rule-based international order post-2022. This has both principled and strategic dimensions — as a new NATO member, Sweden benefits from demonstrating commitment to collective Western Ukraine policy.

**Russian reaction**: Russia has declared this a "hostile act." Sweden's SÄPO and MUST assess the elevated hybrid threat risk.
