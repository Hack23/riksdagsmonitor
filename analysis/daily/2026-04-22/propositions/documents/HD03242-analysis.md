## Analysis

Active Forestry Regulatory Framework (2025/26:242):

- **Forestry rights**: Clarifies and strengthens property owners' right to manage forests actively (active silviculture vs passive conservation)
- **EU tension**: Addresses conflict between Swedish forestry tradition and EU Nature Restoration Regulation requirements
- **Biodiversity measures**: Includes targeted biodiversity measures to maintain environmental credentials while enabling active management
- **Swedish forest industry**: Significant economic interests (IKEA supply chain, pulp & paper, bioenergy)

**Why it matters**: Sweden's forest sector generates approximately 4% of GDP and is a major export earner. EU nature restoration requirements threatened to restrict logging in biodiversity-rich areas. This proposition attempts to find a Swedish solution that satisfies EU law while protecting the industry.

**Political dynamics**: C (Centre Party) is the natural advocate for rural landowners and farmers — Peter Kullgren's authorship reflects this. The urban-rural divide is visible in attitudes toward this legislation.

**EU risk**: If the proposition's forestry exemptions are found incompatible with the Nature Restoration Law, Sweden faces infringement proceedings.
