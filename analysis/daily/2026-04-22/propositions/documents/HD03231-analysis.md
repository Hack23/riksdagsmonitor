## Analysis

Sweden joins the Special Tribunal for the Crime of Aggression against Ukraine (2025/26:231):

- **Tribunal mandate**: The tribunal will prosecute those responsible for the crime of aggression against Ukraine (primarily Russian leadership)
- **Council of Europe partial agreement**: Sweden joins the expanded framework alongside 40+ other states
- **Complementarity**: Works alongside ICC proceedings; fills accountability gap since Russia is not ICC member
- **Sweden's role**: Active participating state; contributes to tribunal governance and funding

**Why it matters**: This is a landmark international law development — the first dedicated aggression tribunal since Nuremberg. Sweden's participation signals that aggressive war has criminal consequences. The tribunal will be watching the fate of captured Russian soldiers and potentially Putin-era leadership if political conditions change.

**Strategic significance**: Alongside HD03232, Sweden becomes part of the international accountability architecture that Russia will face post-conflict. This has long-term implications for Sweden's standing in European security order.
