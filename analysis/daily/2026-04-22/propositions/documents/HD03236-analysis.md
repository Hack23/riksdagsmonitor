## Analysis

The Extra Amendment Budget (2025/26:236) targets household cost-of-living through two mechanisms:

1. **Fuel tax reduction** (sänkt skatt på drivmedel): Reduction in the energy tax on petrol and diesel — specific amounts in the proposition
2. **Electricity and gas price support** (el- och gasprisstöd): Targeted support to households facing high energy bills

**Political context**: This measure was signed by Acting PM Lotta Edholm (L) and Finance Minister Niklas Wykman (M) on 13 April 2026. The fuel tax cut is politically contentious because:
- Miljöpartiet will call it "climate betrayal" (klimatförräderi)
- It benefits rural, car-dependent voters — a key target constituency for M, C, SD
- It potentially conflicts with Sweden's 2030 climate commitments

**Why it matters**: This is the most visible pre-election household relief measure in the spring package. Combined with HD03100 and HD0399, it shows the government willing to use fiscal space for cost-of-living responses.
