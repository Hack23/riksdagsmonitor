## Analysis

The Wind Power in Municipalities proposition (2025/26:239) introduces a mandatory revenue-sharing model:

- **Intäktsdelning** (revenue sharing): Wind power operators must share a defined percentage of revenues with the municipality where turbines are located
- **Local compensation fund**: Revenue used for infrastructure, local services, or direct payments to affected residents
- **Municipal consent**: The komunalt veto on wind projects is not removed but the economic incentive changes the calculus

**Why it matters**: Municipal opposition to wind farms has been one of the primary barriers to Sweden meeting its renewable energy targets. Opinion surveys show municipalities more willing to approve projects when they receive direct economic benefit. This proposition attempts to replicate the "community benefit" model successful in Denmark.

**Political dynamics**: KD (Johan Britz) pushed this as alternative to removing the municipal veto; M supports; SD has been sceptical of wind generally but may accept with compensation; MP and S likely to support but may argue the amounts are too low.
