## Analysis

Paid Police Training (2025/26:237) converts the current unpaid police academy model:

- **Salaried training**: Police cadets receive salary during their 2.5-year police academy education (currently the training involves only limited financial support)
- **Increased intake**: Higher salary expected to attract more applicants, allowing increased annual intake
- **Target**: Government aims to significantly increase police density per capita

**Why it matters**: Sweden has approximately 220 police officers per 100,000 population (below the EU average of ~320). The paid training model is intended to accelerate the expansion programme that began with the 2017 Polisreform. The proposition builds on successful precedents from the military's paid basic training (värnplikten with pay from 2017).

**Cost**: The Vårändringsbudget (HD0399) provides initial funding; ongoing costs in the annual budget.
