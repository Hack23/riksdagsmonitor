## Analysis

The New Environmental Review Authority proposition (2025/26:238) creates a dedicated national agency for environmental permitting of industrial and energy projects:

- **New myndighet**: Replaces the fragmented system where Länsstyrelserna (county administrative boards) handle major permits
- **Faster processing**: Target processing times reduced from 5-7 years to 2-3 years for major projects
- **Specialised expertise**: Dedicated staff with environmental law and engineering expertise
- **Appeals consolidation**: Streamlined appeal pathway to reduce litigation delays

**Why it matters**: Sweden's industrial expansion plans (LKAB steel, Northvolt gigafactory, new nuclear sites) have been delayed by complex permitting processes. This reform directly addresses the competitiveness bottleneck.

**Implementation risk**: Transition period where both old and new systems operate simultaneously creates processing gap risk. New agency needs 150-200 FTE from day one.
