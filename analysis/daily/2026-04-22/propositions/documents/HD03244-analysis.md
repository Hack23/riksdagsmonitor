## Analysis

Data Interoperability Requirements for Public Administration (2025/26:244):

- **Interoperability mandate**: New requirements for government agencies to use standardised data formats and APIs for data sharing
- **EU alignment**: Implements EU Interoperability Act requirements
- **Benefits**: Faster cross-agency processes (e.g., social security, tax, healthcare)
- **Timeline**: Phased implementation 2027-2030

**Why it matters**: Sweden's public administration efficiency depends on seamless data sharing between 350+ government agencies. This proposition creates the legal foundation for the next generation of digital government services.
