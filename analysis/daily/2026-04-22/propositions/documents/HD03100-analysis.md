## Analysis

The 2026 Economic Spring Proposition (2025/26:100) is the Kristersson government's primary pre-election fiscal statement. It sets the riktlinjer (guidelines) for Sweden's fiscal policy for 2027 and beyond, within the constraints of the surplus target (överskottsmål, +1/3% of GDP) and the debt anchor. Key elements:

- **Growth outlook**: Government forecasts 1.8% GDP growth for 2026, above OECD's 1.4% estimate
- **Surplus target**: Maintained within acceptable range; room for some household relief measures
- **Reform priorities**: Continued labour market activation; industrial competitiveness; energy transition support
- **Political context**: First spring proposition before the September 2026 election — this is effectively the government's economic manifesto

**Why it matters**: In a Swedish election year, the vårproposition is the key policy document. Opposition (S, MP, V) will use the committee process to critique growth assumptions and advocate alternative spending priorities. The FiU (Finance Committee) vote is expected in May 2026.

**Election 2026 lens**: The surplus headroom in HD03100 enables the HD03236 fuel tax package — the government is strategically sequencing "fiscal discipline + targeted relief."

**Confidence**: [B2] — Based on published proposition and established fiscal framework patterns.
