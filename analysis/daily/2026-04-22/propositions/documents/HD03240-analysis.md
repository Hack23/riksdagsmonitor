## Analysis

The New Electricity System Laws proposition (2025/26:240) represents the most comprehensive reform of Sweden's electricity legislation since the market liberalisation in the 1990s. The proposition creates an updated framework governing:

- **Grid connection rights**: New rules for connecting generation and consumption to the grid, including priority access for industrial electrification projects
- **Market balancing**: Updated real-time market rules as Sweden integrates more variable renewables
- **Consumer protection**: Strengthened rules for retail electricity contracts and supplier failure protection
- **Cross-border integration**: Updated rules for Nordic/European electricity market participation post-Brexit adjustments

**Why it matters**: Sweden is in the midst of a major energy transition — planned new nuclear reactors, massive data centre investment, EV fleet growth, and green steel production all require a modernised legal framework. The old Ellag (1997) was designed for a different grid.

**Co-signer note**: Signed by Lotta Edholm as acting PM and Elisabet Lann (KD) — KD has led the nuclear expansion agenda within the coalition.

**Election 2026 lens**: Competitiveness-enabling legislation — positions Sweden as destination for energy-intensive industry.
