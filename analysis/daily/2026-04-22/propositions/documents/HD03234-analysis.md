## Analysis

New Municipal Port Operations Law (2025/26:234):

- **Framework**: Creates a new dedicated law for municipal port operations replacing scattered provisions
- **Competition rules**: Ports must operate under transparent, non-discriminatory access rules
- **EU port regulations**: Implements EU Port Services Regulation requirements
- **Governance**: Clarifies ownership and accountability structure for municipal ports

**Why it matters**: Sweden's 50+ commercial ports are critical for import/export (particularly for bulky goods like steel, timber, minerals). Clearer legal framework reduces legal uncertainty for port investments.
