## Key Findings Summary

1. **HD03100 (Vårpropositionen)** is the anchor document — the spring budget defines the government's economic recovery narrative 5 months before the election
2. **HD03236 (emergency relief)** is the SD-facing concession in the coalition, passed via an extra ändringsbudget
3. **Energy trinity** (HD03238+HD03239+HD03240) forms a strategic infrastructure reform package with broad cross-party support
4. **Security triple** (HD03237+HD03246+HD03252) targets the election's primary battleground issue
5. **Coalition margin** is 1 seat (176/349) — single-seat-majority legislative risk on 4 of 8 key votes
