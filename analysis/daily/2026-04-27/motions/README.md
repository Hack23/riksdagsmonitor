## Overview

This directory contains the full political intelligence analysis of Swedish Riksdag opposition motions for the period April 7–17, 2026 (riksmöte 2025/26). Analysis covers 29 valid motions (HD024068–HD024098, HD024071 excluded — "Motionen utgår") filed across 10 committees in response to the Tidö government's spring legislative programme.

## Key Finding

**Opposition motions will fail legislatively but succeed electorally.** The 29 motions cluster around three themes: immigration rights (28%), fiscal policy (17%), and housing/integration (14%). All will be rejected by the Tidö majority with SD support. Their primary value is electoral positioning ahead of the September 2026 general election.
