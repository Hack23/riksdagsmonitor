**Tertiary Story**: The Energy Transition Package (HD03240 + HD03239 + HD03238) restructures Sweden's electricity market architecture, creates a new permitting authority, and introduces mandatory revenue-sharing for wind power — the latter a significant redistribution toward municipalities hosting turbines.
