## Lead Intelligence

**BLUF**: The Tidöalliansen has tabled Sweden's most comprehensive migration restriction package since 1994. Four propositions (HD03262–65) simultaneously abolish permanent residence permits, expand deportation, introduce character vetting, and extend detention. Combined with NATO defence integration (HD03254) and ratified economic framework (HC01FiU20), this is the government's pre-election legislative strategy. The critical chokepoint for the week of 4–10 May is Lagrådet's pending ECHR opinion on HD03262/HD03265 — the outcome determines whether this is an electoral triumph or constitutional crisis.
