## 📍 What This Dossier Covers

A coordinated Kristersson-government legislative sprint published 2026-04-13 – 2026-04-16, three weeks into Q2 2026 and five months before the 2026-09-13 general election:

1. **Vårproposition 2026 (HD03100)** — annual spring fiscal framework; DIW 9.5 (#1 lead)
2. **Extra Ändringsbudget — fuel & energy relief (HD03236)** — extraordinary supplementary budget; DIW 8.5
3. **Skärpta regler unga lagöverträdare (HD03246)** — youth-offender toughening (15–17 year-olds); DIW 7.5
4. **Inhibition av verkställigheten (HD01SfU22)** — migration inhibition orders replacing temporary permits; DIW 6.5

Together these four documents form a **cost-of-living + security + macro-competence** pre-election platform with measurable operational (SiS), legal (ECHR Art. 3), and fiscal-credibility (Riksrevisionen) exposure.

## 🔑 Top-Line Findings (Copy-Paste Safe)

1. **Vårproposition 2026 (HD03100) is the year's defining fiscal document** — fragile recovery (GDP +0.82% 2024 after −0.20% 2023), inflation tamed (2.84% 2024 from 8.55% 2023), but unemployment at 8.7% (2025 ≈ 450,000 individuals) is the coalition's core electoral vulnerability. `[HIGH]`
2. **The SiS (Statens institutionsstyrelse) capacity crisis is the single highest-severity operational risk** — youth detention has been at 100%+ throughout 2025; HD03246 will increase demand by an estimated 15–20% without a corresponding capital investment. `[HIGH]`
3. **HD01SfU22 carries material ECHR Article 3 litigation exposure** — mandatory geographic restriction and reporting for deportation-blocked individuals without automatic judicial review parallels structures the ECtHR has previously found incompatible with Article 3 and Article 5 §1(f). `[MEDIUM]`
4. **Three mini-budgets in two months (HD03100 + HD0399 + HD03236) signal fiscal-framework stress** — Riksrevisionen's own report on fiscal-framework application (HD03241) is now the reference benchmark; frequent mid-year adjustments attract overshoot commentary. `[MEDIUM]`
5. **All four documents are pre-election positioning** — cost-of-living (energy subsidies), security (youth crime + migration), macro-competence (vårproposition) maps precisely onto the 2026 election's top-3 voter-salience issues (SVT/Ipsos 2026-Q1). `[HIGH]`
6. **International benchmarking exposes the interpretive-frontier nodes** — see `comparative-international.md` for how Germany, Denmark, Finland, UK, and the Netherlands have resolved the same fiscal / youth-justice / migration-inhibition design choices Sweden is now making. `[MEDIUM]`
