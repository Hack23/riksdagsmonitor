## Headline Findings

### 1. Security-State Consolidation Is Near-Complete
The Tidö government has enacted Sweden's most comprehensive security reform package in a generation. HD01JuU32 (event security), HD03267 (qualified security threats — foreign nationals), HD01JuU34 (Nordic criminal enforcement), HD01JuU39 (psychological violence), and HD03263 (return enforcement) collectively represent a structural shift in Sweden's security architecture. These laws will be difficult for any successor government to reverse. **Forward-probability**: 85% these laws remain intact through 2027-05-07 regardless of election outcome.

### 2. Digital Sovereignty Is Now Statutory
HD03250 (En statlig e-legitimation) creates a new state e-ID infrastructure managed by a designated authority. Combined with HD03261 (expanded Skatteverket population registry powers), Sweden is building a state-centric digital identity stack. This will reshape government-citizen interaction, voter registration verification, and welfare payment integrity. **Implementation risk**: High (timeline T+12–24 months for full rollout; Lagrådet referral pending as of 2026-05-09).

### 3. Financial Architecture Rebuilt for New Risks
HD01FiU37 creates a new operational crisis management function in Sweden's financial sector — a BRRD-adjacent mechanism enabling coordinated response to cyber-attacks, infrastructure failures, or systemic stress events. HD01FiU38 extends EU clearing obligation to Swedish markets. Together they address the financial stability gap exposed by the 2023 European banking mini-crisis and 2024 Baltic cyber incidents. **Relevance horizon**: Critical at T+12 (new government must staff and operationalise).

### 4. Election Outcome Drives Divergent Post-2026 Trajectories
Four coalition configurations remain genuinely possible (see coalition-mathematics.md). Under Scenario A (Tidö continuation), security consolidation accelerates and e-ID implementation stays on track. Under Scenario B (S-led bloc victory), security laws are maintained but immigration enforcement softens. Under Scenario C (S+C+L rainbow), digital governance reform may be delayed. Under Scenario D (hung Riksdag/minority coalition), implementation feasibility for complex legislation drops sharply. **Probability distribution**: A=32%, B=38%, C=18%, D=12% as of 2026-05-09.
