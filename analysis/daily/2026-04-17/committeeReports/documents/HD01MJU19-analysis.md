## 6-Lens Analysis

### Lens 1: Policy Substance [HIGH]
Proposition 2025/26:108 reforms Sweden's waste legislation to align with the EU circular economy package. Key changes in miljöbalken (Environmental Code):

1. **New waste responsibility rules** — clarifying when responsibility for waste begins/ends along value chain; detailhandel (retail) facing new obligations
2. **Material recycling targets** — adopting EU minimum targets (but NOT exceeding them, as per government philosophy)
3. **Supervision and illegal dumping** — new enforcement mechanisms for illegal waste activity
4. **Future evaluation mechanism** — government proposes biannual reporting but without mandatory legislative review

The legal package also amends: the Ship Pollution Prevention Act (1980:424), the Swedish Economic Zone Act (1992:1140), and the Radiation Safety Act (2018:396).

### Lens 4: EU Context [HIGH]
Sweden's waste reform implements:
- EU Waste Framework Directive revision (Directive 2018/851)
- EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) preparatory alignment
- Circular Economy Action Plan (2020) national transposition

Sweden is **late** in implementing some elements — the EU had flagged Sweden in 2024 monitoring for lagging material recycling rates. This legislation is partly forced by EU compliance necessity, not domestic political leadership.
