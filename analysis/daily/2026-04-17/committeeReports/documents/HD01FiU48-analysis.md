## 6-Lens Analysis

### Lens 1: Policy Substance [MEDIUM]
The extra amendment budget (tilläggsbudget) for 2026 combines:
1. **Fuel tax reduction** — lowering the energy tax on petrol and diesel to reduce pump prices for Swedish consumers
2. **Electricity and gas price support** — direct subsidy mechanism for households facing elevated energy bills

This follows a pattern of emergency fiscal interventions first used during the 2021–2023 energy price crisis. The government is deploying two tools simultaneously: a supply-side tax cut (fuel) and a demand-side subsidy (electricity/gas support). Combined, these represent the government's response to sustained cost-of-living pressure on Swedish households entering the 2026 election year.
