# Per-Document Analysis: HD03242

**Title**: Ett tydligt regelverk för aktivt skogsbruk  
**Prop. Number**: 2025/26:242  
**Minister**: Peter Kullgren (C, Landsbygds- och infrastrukturdepartementet)  
**Committee**: Miljö- och jordbruksutskottet (MJU)  
**Date**: 2026-04-16  
**Significance**: 7/10 (🟧 MEDIUM confidence — contested legal ground)

## Policy Summary

Proposition 2025/26:242 establishes a new regulatory framework for "active forestry management" in Sweden, aiming to clarify when and how forest owners can conduct harvesting, drainage, and land management activities in relation to species protection requirements. Key changes:

1. **Clarified permit process**: Replaces complex case-by-case species assessments with pre-defined zones and activity protocols
2. **"Aktiv skötsel" presumption**: Active management is presumed compatible with conservation unless specific indicators trigger detailed assessment
3. **Streamlined compensation**: Clear rules for when forest owners are entitled to compensation for species protection restrictions (following HD03230)
4. **Implementation via Skogsstyrelsen**: The Forest Agency gets clearer guidance to reduce discretionary decisions

## Why It Matters

Swedish forestry is a SEK 100 billion+ annual industry employing ~65,000 people directly and supporting hundreds of thousands in rural communities. Since the EU's stricter interpretation of the Habitats Directive came into Swedish court practice around 2020-2021, forest owners have faced increasing legal uncertainty about their management rights.

The proposition reflects a fundamental political choice: the Kristersson government, under C-party leadership in Landsbygdsdepartementet, has chosen to resolve this uncertainty in favor of active management rather than passive conservation.

## EU Legal Risk

This is the most legally contested element of the package. Sweden has already received informal concerns from the EU Commission regarding implementation of the Habitats Directive. HD03242 may trigger:

1. **EU Pilot**: Pre-infringement dialogue
2. **Article 258 TFEU infringement**: If Commission believes HD03242 systematically weakens species protection
3. **ECJ Reference**: In extreme case, European Court of Justice ruling requiring Sweden to amend or repeal

Naturvårdsverket's formal remiss opinion (expected in committee process) will be the clearest indicator of EU compliance risk.

## Political Significance for C Party

This is Centerpartiet's most significant rural victory in the 2025/26 legislative year. C has been squeezed by SD on immigration and by M on urban economy — HD03242 reminds C's traditional farm/forest owner base that the party delivers. If the proposition passes before September 2026, C can credibly claim it delivered on rural Sweden's demands.
