3. **Forestry Deregulation (HD03242) — Significance 7/10**: Landsbygdsminister Peter Kullgren's Prop. 2025/26:242 rewrites Sweden's forestry management rules to prioritize active management over passive conservation — directly challenging EU Habitats Directive interpretation and energizing both rural voters and environmental opposition.

4. **Digital Governance (HD03244) — Significance 7/10**: Prop. 2025/26:244 from Finansminister Erik Slottner implements EU Data Governance Act requirements, mandating that all Swedish government agencies adopt open API standards for data sharing by 2028.

## Political Significance Assessment

### Pre-Election Legislative Strategy

The April 16 package is the most revealing indicator yet of the Kristersson government's election strategy. Five propositions were tabled simultaneously across Justice, Foreign Affairs, Finance, and Landsbygd — each tailored to different coalition constituencies:

| Proposition | Primary Audience | Coalition Partner |
|------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| HD03246 Youth | Urban/suburban crime-worried voters | M + SD |
| HD03231/232 Ukraine | NATO/Western values voters | M + KD |
| HD03244 Digital | Business/efficiency voters | KD |
| HD03242 Forestry | Rural landowners | C + SD |

This is textbook coalition management — every partner gets a visible win, all in the same news cycle.

### Legislative Timeline Analysis

With 5 months to September 2026 election:
- **HD03246**: Needs to clear JuU by June for summer chamber vote — tight
- **HD03231/232**: UU can fast-track; cross-party consensus; target: May 2026
- **HD03244**: TU review through summer; implementation 2028 — no election pressure
- **HD03242**: MJU contested; may not pass before election (becomes campaign promise)

**Critical observation**: The government may be tabling HD03242 knowing it won't pass before election — instead using the proposed forestry framework as a campaign mobilization tool for rural C/SD voters.
