## Document Summary

Independent MP Jamal El-Haj demands that Sweden's government take three specific actions regarding the 1948 assassination of Swedish UN mediator Folke Bernadotte by the Lehi/Stern Gang (a Zionist paramilitary organization):
1. Demand that Israel formally acknowledge state responsibility for the assassination
2. Demand a formal public apology from Israel to the Bernadotte family and Sweden
3. Demand financial compensation from Israel for the Bernadotte family

The interpellation documents:
- The attack was carried out by Lehi (also known as Sternligan/Fighters for the Freedom of Israel)
- Four assassins were identified; three confessed in Swedish media interviews
- The operational commander (Yitzhak Shamir) later became Israeli Prime Minister (1983–1984, 1986–1992) under Likud
- The killers were never prosecuted; one received a military decoration for "contributing to founding the state of Israel"
- No formal apology or compensation has been paid to Sweden or the Bernadotte family in 78 years

The interpellation explicitly connects this historical case to contemporary issues:
- Israel's newly adopted death penalty laws (criticized by Stenergard herself)
- The Gaza conflict and occupation
- Questions about selective justice (military courts for Palestinians vs civil courts for Israelis)
