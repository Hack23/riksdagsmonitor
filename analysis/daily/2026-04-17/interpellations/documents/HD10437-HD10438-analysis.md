## HD10437: Lönetransparensdirektivet (Pay Transparency Directive)

**Core Claim**: Sweden will not implement the EU Pay Transparency Directive on time because the government withdrew its own implementation proposal.

**Key Evidence from Interpellation Text**:
- Sweden's wage gap is "persistent and has even increased in recent years"
- EU's Pay Transparency Directive is designed to address structural disadvantage of women through new legislation
- "The government's proposal for implementing the directive in Swedish law has been withdrawn"
- "Sweden will not succeed in implementing the directive on time"

**Legal Context**: The EU Pay Transparency Directive requires member states to implement legislation ensuring:
- Workers' right to salary information
- Employers' reporting obligations on gender pay gaps
- Judicial remedies for equal pay violations

Sweden missing the implementation deadline means:
- Swedish women lose access to new EU-mandated pay transparency protections
- Swedish businesses face legal uncertainty about compliance requirements
- EU Commission may open infringement proceedings against Sweden

**Accountability Gap**: [HIGH CONFIDENCE] The government cannot deny withdrawing its implementation proposal — this is a documented parliamentary record. The question is only about why and when/if the proposal will be resubmitted.

## HD10438: Nedläggning av kvinnojourer (Women's Shelter Closures)

**Core Claim**: Women's shelters (jourer) are closing across Sweden, undermining the civil society infrastructure for combating men's violence against women.

**Key Evidence from Interpellation Text**:
- Civil society organizations for women's rights work (kvinnofridsarbetet) are "important and fundamental"
- "Many women's shelters around the country are being closed"
- Closures have "major consequences for the ability to leave a violent relationship"

**Background Context**:
- Women's shelters (jourer) in Sweden rely on a mix of state, municipal, and private funding
- Under Tidö coalition's fiscal consolidation, municipal budgets have been squeezed
- Municipal cuts cascade to NGO funding — jourer are often first affected
- This pattern has been documented by the National Board of Health and Welfare (Socialstyrelsen)

**Impact Analysis**: [HIGH CONFIDENCE]
- Physical infrastructure for domestic violence victims is closing
- Women in violent relationships lose access to emergency accommodation and support
- The welfare state's most visible protection mechanism for vulnerable women is visibly degrading
- Each closed shelter is a local news story — multiplicative media effect
