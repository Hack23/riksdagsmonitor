## Critical Risk: Gender Equality Cluster (R1+R2 combined = 31 points)

The combination of pay transparency failure AND women's shelter closures creates a compound risk for Nina Larsson (L) and the Tidö coalition:
- **EU Compliance Risk** [HIGH CONFIDENCE]: Sweden has definitively missed the Pay Transparency Directive implementation deadline. The government withdrew its own legislative proposal. This is not a contested claim — it is an established fact, confirmed by the interpellation text referencing the withdrawn legislation.
- **Civil Society Infrastructure Collapse** [MEDIUM CONFIDENCE]: Reports of women's shelter (jourer) closures across Sweden are credible given the general squeeze on civil society funding under the Tidö coalition's fiscal consolidation program.
- **Combined electoral narrative**: "Government has abandoned women" — high resonance in constituencies where S and MP compete for female voters.

## Critical Risk: Israel/Palestine (R3 = 16 points)

The Bernadotte interpellation (HD10435) carries unusually high risk because:
- **Maria Malmer Stenergard's dilemma**: She has already criticized Israel's death penalty laws, but this interpellation demands concrete action (apology demand, compensation). Any response that falls short will be used by both the left ("not enough") and potentially by pro-Israel voices ("too much").
- **Historical irrefutability**: The facts about Bernadotte's assassination are not contested. Three of the four identified perpetrators confessed. Yitzhak Shamir became PM. This creates a legally solid accountability demand.
- **Gaza conflict amplification**: The interpellation explicitly links historical impunity to current events (Gaza conflict, death penalty laws). This is a powerful rhetorical move that the minister cannot easily deflect.
- **Voter mobilization signal** [MEDIUM CONFIDENCE]: Filed by independent MP with connections to Muslim/Arab-Swedish community. Timing (6 months pre-election) suggests organized voter mobilization effort.
