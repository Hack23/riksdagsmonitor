[LOW CONFIDENCE on full resolution]

## THREATS (External & Electoral Threats)

### T1 — Pre-Election Media Amplification [HIGH — 🟩HIGH CONFIDENCE]
With September 2026 election, every interpellation now has enhanced news value. Media will cover gender equality and women's shelter stories as election-cycle accountability journalism. The opposition's interpellation campaign gets free amplification.

### T2 — EU Infringement Proceedings [MEDIUM — 🟧MEDIUM CONFIDENCE]
If the Pay Transparency Directive non-compliance is not resolved quickly, the European Commission may open formal infringement proceedings before the election. This would be a devastating news story: "Sweden taken to court by EU over women's pay rights."

### T3 — Voter Coalition Fragmentation [HIGH — 🟩HIGH CONFIDENCE]
Independent MP Jamal El-Haj's Bernadotte interpellation is a signal of Muslim/Arab-Swedish voter mobilization around Palestine/Israel issues. If this community (historically S-aligned) votes on this single issue, it could affect marginal constituencies. The government's response will be scrutinized.

### T4 — Cascading Infrastructure Failures [HIGH — 🟩HIGH CONFIDENCE]
Each new infrastructure failure (train closure, road safety incident, housing project collapse) generates another interpellation. Without concrete improvements visible to voters, the interpellation campaign against Carlson (KD) will continue to grow in intensity.

### T5 — Civil Society Alienation [MEDIUM — 🟧MEDIUM CONFIDENCE]
As women's shelters close and other NGOs face funding cuts, the civil society sector (historically an S voter recruitment ground) becomes mobilized against the Tidö government. This has multiplicative electoral effects through organizational networks.

### T6 — Coalition Internal Strain [LOW-MEDIUM — 🟥LOW CONFIDENCE]
C's LGBTQ+ challenge (HD10431) is manageable but represents a pattern: if C files increasingly pointed interpellations, it signals the cooperation is under stress. Worth monitoring.
