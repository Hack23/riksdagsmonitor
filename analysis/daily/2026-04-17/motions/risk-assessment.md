## Top Risk Scenarios

### Scenario A: Prop. 235 Passes Unchanged (L×I = 16)
**Probability**: High (government+SD majority sufficient)
**Timeline**: SfU committee report expected May-June 2026; chamber vote before summer recess
**Trigger for escalation**: European Court of Human Rights case within 24 months if law leads to deportation of long-term residents
**Mitigation**: C's amendment (mot. 4095) could be adopted as minor modification — government may accept "systematic repeated crimes" clause to reduce legal risk without surrendering core provision

### Scenario B: Fuel Tax Cut Passes, Opposition Parties Fail to Prevent (L×I = 16)
**Probability**: Near-certain (extra budget has government majority)
**Timeline**: FiU report and chamber vote likely May 2026
**Trigger for escalation**: Post-enactment cost-of-living analysis shows fuel tax cut benefits high-income car owners disproportionately — becomes 2026 election ammunition
**Mitigation**: None available through legislative channels; opposition parties will pivot to electoral campaign framing

### Scenario C: Arms Export Modernisation Passes Without Dictatorship Restrictions (L×I = 12)
**Probability**: High (UU government majority)
**Timeline**: UU committee spring 2026
**Trigger for escalation**: Swedish weapons documented in use by authoritarian state post-enactment
**Mitigation**: MP's targeted motion (4096) could be partially adopted as non-binding declaration in UU report
