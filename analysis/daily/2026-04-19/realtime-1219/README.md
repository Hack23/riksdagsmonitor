## 📍 Why This Run Is a Reference Exemplar

This realtime-1219 dossier is explicitly constructed to meet the **14-artifact Tier-C reference-grade bar** previously reserved for aggregation workflows (week-ahead, month-ahead, evening-analysis, weekly-review, monthly-review). The rationale: *realtime-monitor is the flagship editorial surface of Riksdagsmonitor* — every breaking run is consumed by editors, analysts, and external press and must therefore carry the same decision-maker entry points, scenario analysis, comparative benchmarking, and methodology self-audit as a weekly review.

The extension addresses two failure modes in the initial 1219 draft:

1. **Shallow-artefact failure** — core artefacts were 1/3 to 1/5 the size of the 1434 canonical exemplar; analyst confidence labels, Bayesian updates, and international benchmarking were only gestured at.
2. **Missing Tier-C entry points** — no `README.md`, `executive-brief.md`, `scenario-analysis.md`, `comparative-international.md`, or `methodology-reflection.md` meant editors had to read the full 9-file registry before forming a lead-story judgement.

Discretionary reading ⇒ systemic press-freedom chilling. Lagrådet's proportionality determination is the decisive pre-vote signal. `[HIGH]`
3. **HD03231 (Special Tribunal) is the first aggression-crime tribunal since Nuremberg (1946).** Sweden joins ≈ 40 states filling the ICC jurisdictional gap (Russia non-member). `[HIGH]`
4. **HD03232 (Compensation Commission) carries no direct Swedish fiscal burden for reparations** — payments sourced from Russian immobilised assets (≈ €260bn, €191bn at Euroclear). Swedish contribution is administrative (≈ SEK 50–200m/yr projected). `[HIGH]`
5. **Cross-cluster rhetorical tension** between *narrowing* domestic transparency (KU33) and *advancing* international accountability (Ukraine package) is the analytical centre of this run and an opposition-exploitable campaign theme for September 2026. `[HIGH]`
6. **King Carl Gustaf + FM Malmer Stenergard's Kyiv visit on 2026-04-17** — the day after HD03231/HD03232 were tabled — is a deliberate royal signal elevating the Ukraine package above ordinary foreign-policy routine. `[HIGH]`
