**April 2026 is the single most electorally consequential legislative month of the 2025/26 Riksdag session.** The Tidö-koalitionen (M–SD–KD–L) concentrated its identity-defining bills into a pre-election sprint:

- **Spring Fiscal Trilogy** — `HD03100` Vårproposition + `HD0399` Vårändringsbudget + `HD03236` Extra ändringsbudget (≈ SEK 60 B net stimulus; 82 öre/litre fuel-tax cut)
- **Criminal-justice bloc** — `HD03218` double penalties + `HD03217` civil-servant liability + `HD03246` youth-offender rules + `HD03245` men's-violence strategy + `HD03237` paid police education
- **Constitutional pair (vilande)** — `HD01KU32` media accessibility + `HD01KU33` digital-evidence search/seizure (second reading by post-Sep-2026 Riksdag)
- **NATO operational first** — `HD03220` 1,200 Swedish troops to Finland under enhanced Forward Presence
- **Environmental deregulation** — `HD03238` new permit authority + `HD03242` active forestry + `HD03239` wind-power municipal veto + `HD03240` new electricity-system law
- **International justice** — `HD03231` Ukraine Crime-of-Aggression Tribunal + `HD03232` International Compensation Commission
