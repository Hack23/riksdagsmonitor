## SITUATION

Sweden's Riksdag is in the legislative peak of the 2025/26 parliamentary session. The Tidö-koalitionen government (M–SD–KD–L) has concentrated its most consequential legislation in a four-week period ahead of the September 2026 election.

---

## KEY FACTS

- **4 budget propositions** tabled in one week (April 13–14) — spring economic strategy
- **Fuel tax cut + energy price support** (HD03236) — direct pre-election household relief
- **Criminal justice overhaul** — double penalties (HD03218), youth offender rules (HD03246), civil servant liability (HD03217)
- **NATO Finland contribution** (HD03220) — first explicit NATO Article 5 commitment
- **Environmental deregulation** — new permit agency (HD03238), active forestry (HD03242)
- **Women's shelter crisis** — interpellation filed as shelters close nationwide (HD10438)
