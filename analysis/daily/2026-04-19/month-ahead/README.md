## 🎯 Purpose of This Package

This is a **reference-grade monthly strategic outlook** covering the Riksdag's **final pre-recess sprint** before the September 2026 general election. The horizon is deliberately bracketed at 19 May 2026 because the Riksdag's effective voting window closes by **mid-June** (before summer recess), compressing ≈ 20 consequential votes into ~4 weeks.

The package aggregates five interlocking legislative clusters into a single forward-looking political-intelligence picture:

1. **Spring Fiscal Trilogy** (`HD03100` Vårproposition + `HD0399` Vårändringsbudget + `HD03236` Extra ändringsbudget — fuel-tax cut + el/gas relief)
2. **Migration / Criminal-Justice Blitz** (`HD03229` reception law + `HD03235` deportation rules + `HD01SfU22` inhibition orders + `HD03246` juvenile tightening + `HD03237` paid police training)
3. **Ukraine Accountability Architecture** (`HD03231` Special Tribunal + `HD03232` Reparations Commission + `HD03220`/`HD01UFöU3` NATO eFP Finland)
4. **Energy / Climate Reform** (`HD03240` electricity system laws + `HD03239` wind-power revenue sharing + `HD01SkU23` EV-charging relief + `HD03238` new permitting agency)
5. **Digital & Constitutional Governance** (`HD03244` interoperability + `HD01TU21` state e-ID + `HD01KU32` accessibility + `HD01KU33` digital-evidence — 2nd reading post-election)

Every analytical file:

- Carries an explicit **5-level confidence scale** (⬛ VERY LOW · 🟥 LOW · 🟧 MEDIUM · 🟩 HIGH · 🟦 VERY HIGH)
- Cites **dok_id evidence** for every major claim
- Incorporates **forward indicators from 2026-04-14 → 2026-04-18 upstream daily analyses** (continuity of intelligence)
- Applies **Democratic-Impact Weighting (DIW)** to lead-story selection
- Names a **methodology footer** (specific guide(s) applied)
- Cross-references siblings in this folder and upstream sibling runs
- Concludes with a **next-watchpoint window** with explicit triggers
