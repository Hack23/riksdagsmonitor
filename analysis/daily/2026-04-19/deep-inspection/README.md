## 🔑 Top-Line Findings (Copy-Paste Safe)

1. **Sweden becomes a founding member of the first aggression-crime tribunal since Nuremberg (1945–46).** First criminal court ever to have jurisdiction over the act of starting a war of aggression against a P5-shielded state. `[HIGH]`
2. **Founding-member status qualitatively elevates Sweden's adversary-threat classification in Russian services' targeting taxonomy** — from "Ukraine supporter" (mid-Nordic pattern) toward "tribunal-tier actor" (Baltic-peer pattern). Primary vectors: APT29 (SVR), GRU Sandworm, APT28. `[HIGH]`
3. **Critical policy gap: HD03231 is silent on its own security obligations.** No SÄPO mandate expansion, no NCSC advisory protocol for tribunal communications, no UD data-classification upgrade, no MSB funding uplift, no Försvarsmakten Baltic-cable surveillance budget. This is the single highest-value editorial finding. `[HIGH]`
4. **Primary risk R1 = 20/25 CRITICAL** — Russian cyber targeting of UD/NCSC/Riksdag IT within 24 months of HD03231 ratification. Historic precedent: APT29 targeted ICC staff, systems, and Dutch host infrastructure after the March 2023 Putin arrest warrant. `[HIGH]`
5. **Constitutional two-reading vulnerability window (Sep–Nov 2026)** overlaps the valrörelse window. Russian disinformation operations will target this window most intensively. `[MEDIUM-HIGH]`
6. **Scenario BASE probability = 0.42** — tribunal ratified Q4 2026, sustained below-threshold Russian hybrid pressure, no operational-tier incident before H1 2027 tribunal opening. BULL remediation pathway (security-package follow-on) probability = 0.22. BEAR operational cyber-incident probability = 0.18. `[MEDIUM]`
7. **Defence-industry opportunity**: Saab AB, BAE Systems Bofors, Nammo benefit from sustained Ukraine-reconstruction and EU ReArm procurement signal. EUR 500 B+ cumulative reconstruction market. `[MEDIUM]`
8. **Cross-cluster continuity signal**: HD03231 is the fourth foreign-policy norm-entrepreneurship artefact in Week 16 (with HD03232 reparations commission, HD01UFöU3 NATO eFP Finland 1,200-troop deployment, Stockholm Hague-convention Dec 2025 sign-on).
