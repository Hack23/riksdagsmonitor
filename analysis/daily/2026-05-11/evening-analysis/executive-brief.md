### 1. Constitutional Amendment Advances — Abortion Rights & Terror Restrictions (HIGHEST)
The Riksdag's Constitutional Committee (KU) recommends adopting government proposition 2025/26:78 as a *vilande* (dormant) amendment. Under Sweden's constitutional amendment process (*RF* Chapter 8, §§14–16), a constitutional change requires identical votes in two successive Riksdags with a general election in between. Having passed once (2022/23), this vote finalises the amendment.
