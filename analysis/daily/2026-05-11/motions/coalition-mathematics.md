## Post-2026 Election Coalition Scenarios

**If September 2026 election produces a new left-green majority** (S+V+MP+C):
- Forestry deregulation would likely be revisited/reversed by new government
- Youth crime age-cut provision repealed
- C in a left-green government would demand: significant climate/environment concessions, constitutional governance reform, better Lagrådet compliance rules

**If Tidö bloc retains government** (M+KD+L+SD+potential SD upgrade):
- Both propositions remain in force
- EU Habitats risk managed case-by-case
- Constitutional risk of youth crime bill remains unresolved

**Swing factor**: C's 24 seats are the decisive swing actor. HD024145+HD024146 demonstrate C is genuinely considering either coalition option depending on policy offers.

## HD024146 as Coalition Signal

C's "government pattern of ignoring Lagrådet" framing is not just policy commentary — it's a pre-negotiation list of conditions for any future C coalition partnership:
1. Future government must commit to respecting Lagrådet opinions
2. Future government must conduct proper RF compatibility reviews
3. Future government must not override constitutional advice without exceptional justification

This sets a higher bar for C's right-wing coalition participation than the Tidö coalition currently meets, while setting a lower bar than V/MP would require for governance. C is positioning for the centre.
