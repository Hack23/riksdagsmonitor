## Summary

Sweden stands at a pivotal political juncture. With the 2026 general election 129 days away, the Tidö government is executing a final legislative sprint — pushing through landmark security reforms, digital governance transformation, and financial stability architecture in the closing months of the current Riksdag term. This year-ahead analysis covers the period from May 2026 through May 2027, spanning the election campaign, the September 13 election itself, coalition formation, and the first months of a new government.

Five dominant themes define the year-ahead horizon:

1. **Election 2026** — Sweden's most consequential election since 2006, with a potential historic return of the Social Democrats or a continued Tidö coalition with strengthened SD influence
2. **Security-state transformation** — The Tidö bloc has enacted the most comprehensive security legislation package in Sweden's modern history: event security (JuU32), qualified security threats (HD03267), Nordic enforcement (JuU34), psychological violence (JuU39), and reinforced return procedures (HD03263)
3. **Digital sovereignty** — The state e-ID (HD03250) represents Sweden's foundational digital identity infrastructure, with implications for e-government, tax authority powers (HD03261), and democratic participation
4. **Financial system resilience** — New financial sector crisis management function (FiU37), OTC derivative clearing reform (FiU38), household debt monitoring (HD03255), and state property management (FiU31) collectively build Sweden's post-2025 financial architecture
5. **Post-election realignment** — Coalition mathematics after September 2026 remain highly uncertain; four viable scenarios with divergent policy trajectories mapped in scenario-analysis.md
