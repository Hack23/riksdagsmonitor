## Threat Details

### 1. Accountability Gap Threat

**Severity:** 2/5 (LOW-MEDIUM)
**Sources:** HD01SfU16, HD01FoU8
**Description:** The government has set ambitious targets across multiple policy areas (migration reform implementation, defense personnel expansion, infrastructure investment). Committee reports SfU16 and FoU8 reveal structural challenges in meeting these targets. The gap between political commitments and implementation outcomes creates an accountability deficit that opposition parties can exploit but that also represents a genuine governance risk.

**Evidence:**
- Defense: 10,000+ personnel deficit vs NATO commitments
- Migration: Multiple simultaneous reform bills challenging Migrationsverket capacity
- Infrastructure: SEK 30bn+ deferred rail maintenance

### 2. SD Power Influence Threat

**Severity:** 2/5 (LOW-MEDIUM)
**Source:** HD01SfU16
**Description:** SD as a support party (not formal coalition member) exercises disproportionate influence on migration policy through the Tido Agreement. SfU16 handling of migration motions reflects SD priorities without SD bearing formal ministerial responsibility. This creates a power balance asymmetry where a party without government seats effectively shapes major policy areas.
