### Key Intelligence Refinement: S's JuU30 Vote is the Day's Top Intelligence Signal

The finding that Social Democrats voted **Ja** on JuU30 (imprisoning children 15-17) deserves elevation as the day's single most strategically significant intelligence item — above even the law itself. Here's why:

1. **S is the 2022 election's governing party-in-waiting**: With 94 seats, S leads the opposition. Their Ja vote on JuU30 creates a permanent parliamentary record.

2. **S cannot credibly campaign for reversal**: Having voted to create a law, S faces a logic trap: they would need to argue they were wrong to vote for it, or that circumstances have changed. Both are politically costly.

3. **The opposition's strongest card on crime is weakened**: V and MP (both Nej) now stand alone as the CRC-protecting left. S is implicitly endorsing the government's crime framework.

4. **Swedish political realignment signal**: S's strategic move mirrors Tony Blair's 1994-1997 positioning — moving right on crime to win the centre. This suggests S has internally made peace with the Tidö criminal justice framework and will compete on implementation quality, not principle.

**Revised Key Intelligence Finding**: The most consequential development of 6 May 2026 is not the passage of JuU30 itself, but Social Democrats' decision to vote for it — structurally accepting the Tidö government's criminal justice paradigm and voluntarily surrendering the clearest opposition differentiation point on youth crime.
