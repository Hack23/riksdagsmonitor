## Core Assessment

**What this document does** [B3 — inferred from title + policy context]:
Changes the permit and supervision framework for FOI (Totalförsvarets forskningsinstitut — Swedish Defence Research Institute). FOI conducts defence and security research including dual-use technology. The permit system governs which external entities can commission or access FOI research.

**Significance**: Strengthening civilian oversight of FOI research is part of Sweden's broader post-NATO-accession governance hardening. With increased access to sensitive NATO research streams, FOI's work becomes more sensitive; clearer permit rules reduce the risk of inappropriate access by foreign state-connected actors.
