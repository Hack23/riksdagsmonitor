## Emerging Stakeholder Tension

**BankID vs State E-ID (HD03250)**:  
Swedish commercial banks (whose BankID AB consortium operates the dominant e-ID) face potential market disruption. Banks are unlikely to lobby openly against a state e-ID (politically impossible), but will seek technical standards that ensure BankID compatibility. This tension will play out in TU committee hearings.

**Rural vs Digital Divide (HD03250 + HD10477)**:  
SD's Postnord question (HD10477) and the e-ID proposition (HD03250) create a coalition stress point: SD's rural base faces both Postnord service cuts and digital inclusion challenges. If e-ID is not deployed with adequate analog support, SD constituency satisfaction could erode.
