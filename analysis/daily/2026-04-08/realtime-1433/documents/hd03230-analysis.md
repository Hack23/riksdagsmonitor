## 📝 Executive Summary

Government proposition 2025/26:230 establishes a compensation framework for landowners whose property use is restricted due to species protection regulations. Submitted by PM Svantesson and Minister Britz (L), this balances environmental conservation obligations with property rights — a longstanding tension in Swedish environmental law. The proposition represents a pragmatic L-party priority within the coalition.
