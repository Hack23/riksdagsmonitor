## Executive Summary

The Joint Foreign Affairs and Defence Committee (UFöU) processes Sweden's commitment to deploy up to 1,200 armed forces personnel for NATO's Enhanced Forward Presence (eFP) in Finland through December 31, 2026. This builds on Sweden's broader NATO commitment framework (Prop. 2025/26:6, Prop. 2025/26:25) and represents a deepening of Nordic defence integration within the alliance.
