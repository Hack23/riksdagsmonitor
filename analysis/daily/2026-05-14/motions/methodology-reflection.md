## ≥3 Improvements Identified for Pass 2

### Improvement 1: Lagrådet Opinion Full-Text Retrieval
The Lagrådet's specific opinion on prop 265 was not retrieved in full. The intelligence-assessment KJ-2 and comparative-international ECHR section should ideally cite specific Lagrådet passages. Improvement: Attempt retrieval of Lagrådet opinion if document ID becomes available; if not, add explicit "Lagrådet opinion not retrieved" caveat.

### Improvement 2: ACH Coverage Expansion
The devil's advocate currently covers 4 of 7 key judgments. KJ-5 (party differentiation), KJ-6 (election salience), and KJ-7 (implementation) are not challenged. Pass 2 should expand devils-advocate.md to cover all 7 KJs.

### Improvement 3: S's 2016 Position Reversal Analysis
The cross-reference map notes S's 2016 position reversal but does not analyse the electoral risk that this reversal creates for S. S voters who supported tighter migration in 2016 may not reward a 2026 reversal — this is an analytical gap in the voter-segmentation and election-2026-analysis artifacts.

### Improvement 4: Transport Motions Underdeveloped
Cluster B (transport infrastructure, HD024162–164) is underweighted across all artifacts. The national transport plan 2026–2037 is a significant policy document with a 12-year horizon; its 3 opposition motions deserve more than passing reference.

### Improvement 5: C Party Strategic Analysis Depth
C filed 8 of 15 motions — a dominant footprint. The stakeholder and election-2026 artifacts should better explain *why* C is filing so broadly. Is this a recovery strategy after C's poor 2022 result? What does multi-committee filing tell us about C's target voter groups?

## Methodological Limitations

1. **Data vintage**: All 15 documents date to 2026-05-13 (yesterday); no confirmed committee hearing schedule yet available
2. **No primary source interviews**: Analysis relies entirely on public documents
3. **Economic context**: Migration policy has economic implications (housing, labour market) not captured due to absence of SCB/IMF data for this domain
4. **Predictive limits**: Scenario probabilities are analyst judgment, not derived from systematic base-rate data
